# LarkSuite Pricing Plans (larksuite.com/en_us/plans)

Lark is ByteDance's all-in-one collaboration suite — messaging, calendar, docs, email, video conferencing, approvals, and a no-code platform (Lark Base) in one workspace. International version of Feishu (Lark is the non-China brand; not available in Chinese Mainland/HK/Macau/Japan/South Korea).

## Plans (per-user/month, USD; monthly + annual billing; annual gets discount)
- **Starter (Free)**: $0, up to 20 users, 100 GB storage, core chat/docs/calendar, 5 GB team file storage, 2-person calls. (Some third-party sources cite up to 50 users on the free tier — vendor page says 20.)
- **Basic**: $6/user/mo billed annually (some pages list this; not always surfaced in search excerpts). Up to 500 users, unlimited message history.
- **Pro**: $12/user/mo (billed annually; monthly also available). Up to 500 users, 500-participant video meetings, advanced permissions/automation.
- **Enterprise**: Custom quote. Advanced security, SSO, unlimited users.

Lark is not available in China Mainland/HK/Macau/Japan/South Korea (Feishu serves those). Payments in USD/SGD/JPY depending on region.

## Lark Base (database + automation engine) — priced independently
- Starter: $0 (up to 20 users, 1,000 automations/mo)
- Pro: $8/user/mo (up to 500 users, 50,000 automations/mo)
- Enterprise: quote-based, unlimited users

## Add-ons (flat fees)
- AI Meeting Notes: $1,699 for 1,800 notes
- Meegle Premium (project mgmt): $12/user/mo
- Base automation expansion: $599 per 50,000 automation runs
- AnyCross workflow expansion: $1,000 per 200,000 workflow runs

## Context / positioning
- G2/TrustRadius reviewers: free Starter best for startups/small teams; Pro for growing mid-market needing larger meetings + advanced permissions.
- All-in-one play: competes with Slack+Zoom+Google Workspace+Notion; often cheaper than assembling those separately.
- For comparison: Microsoft 365 Business Premium $22/user/mo (+$18 Copilot); Google Workspace bundles Gemini into base plans in 2026.
