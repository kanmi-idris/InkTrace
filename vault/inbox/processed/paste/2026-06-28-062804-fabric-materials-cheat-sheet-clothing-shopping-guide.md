# Fabric Materials Cheat Sheet — Clothing Shopping Guide

Clothing fabric materials cheat sheet.

AVOID (unless blended well):
- 100% Polyester
- 95% Polyester, 5% Spandex
- Cheap synthetic blends
These are usually thin, shiny, trap heat, build static, feel like swimwear instead of actual clothing.

LOOK FOR:
- Modal (extremely soft)
- Combed Cotton
- Ring-Spun Cotton
- 100% Cotton
- Viscose/Rayon (soft and smooth)

Specific item recommendations:
- Jeans: search "100% Cotton Denim", "Heavyweight Denim", or "Rigid Denim"
- Dress shirts: search "Oxford Cotton", "Cotton Poplin", or "100% Cotton Dress Shirt"
- Jackets: search "Heavyweight", "Fleece Lined", or "Wool Blend"
- Vintage clothes: look for Corduroy, Heavy Knit, or Washed Cotton

Shopping tip: Never trust product photos. Go to reviews, filter by "With Photos", look at pictures from real buyers to see what fabric actually looks and feels like. Look for promos and free/cheap packages.
