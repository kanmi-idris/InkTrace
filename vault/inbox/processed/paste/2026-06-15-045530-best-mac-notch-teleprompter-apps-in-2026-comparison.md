# Best Mac Notch Teleprompter Apps in 2026 - Comparison

Best Mac Notch Teleprompter Apps in 2026: An Honest Comparison
By Steve Chazin (stevechazin.com), published Jun 9, 2026

## Overview
The MacBook notch is the perfect home for a teleprompter - it sits right under the camera so you can read a script while looking almost into the lens. A comparison of 5 Mac notch teleprompter apps as of June 2026.

## What is a MacBook Notch Teleprompter?
A Mac app that parks your script in a thin strip just below the camera notch. Key features:
- Voice-follow scrolling: text advances as you speak, pauses when you stop
- Invisibility: strip is hidden from Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, and screen recordings

## App Details

### CueNotch ($29.99 one-time)
Most feature-packed. Notch placement, on-device voice scrolling, Ghost Mode. AI extras: Magic Polish (script rewrite), AI Rehearsal Coach, AutoCaption (live captions), Memorize Mode. Free tier: 3 activations/day, 100-word limit.

### Moody ($59 one-time)
Most established and mature. Voice-synced scrolling, floating window, manual trackpad scrolling, hover-to-pause, voice-volume beam. On-device only, no cloud. Could not confirm screen-recording invisibility.

### Notchie ($9.99/mo Setapp)
Minimalist. Clean interface, tiny app, voice-activated scrolling offline. Caveats: voice activation can be inconsistent, formatting issues with Markdown. Single language.

### NotchPrompter (Free, open source)
More capable than free usually implies. Scrolls when you speak, invisible to recordings/screen sharing, macOS 14+, Apple Silicon + Intel. Includes multi-monitor support, flexible positioning, OpenDyslexic font.

### Tellie (Free tier, Pro $29 one-time)
Follows voice word-by-word, invisible to Zoom/recorders by default. Key differentiators:
- On-device voice follow in ~50 languages
- Opens: plain text, Markdown, RTF, Word, PDF
- Free developer surface: push line from any app, terminal, or AI agent
- Presenter Mode: mirrors Keynote speaker notes into notch
- Author is Steve Chazin, former Apple executive

## Recommendations
- Best AI help built in: CueNotch
- Best free option: NotchPrompter (open source) or Tellie free tier
- Best for non-English: Tellie (~50 languages)
- Best for Word/PDF imports: Tellie
- Best for Keynote presenters: Tellie Presenter Mode
- Best for developers/AI agents: Tellie (MCP-connected agent support)
- Most mature/polished: Moody ($59)
- Best minimalist on Setapp: Notchie
