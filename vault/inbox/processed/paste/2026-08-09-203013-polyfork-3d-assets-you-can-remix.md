# Polyfork: 3D Assets You Can Remix

## Source overview
Polyfork is a catalogue and remix platform for web-ready 3D assets. The homepage describes each asset as AI-native: a small program made with code rather than a frozen mesh.

## Asset model
Users can adjust asset parameters on the site, in the store, or in downloaded files. The homepage presents a demo with controls for sail span, sail count, and colour palettes.

## Compatibility
The site lists compatibility with three.js, WebXR, Unity, Godot, Blender, and any glTF engine.

## Catalogue and presentation
- The homepage advertises 402 assets.
- It links to free assets, GLB models, low-poly game assets, remixable assets, customizable assets, and parametric assets.
- The site describes its assets as web-ready, reshapeable, flat-shaded, rigged where it matters, and parametric where it counts.
- The homepage includes Browse, Kits, Pricing, Sign in, Remixable, Scene editor, logo and brand assets, performance, support, and an agent guide.
- Polyfork is built by Lucas Martinic and shows a 2026 copyright notice.
