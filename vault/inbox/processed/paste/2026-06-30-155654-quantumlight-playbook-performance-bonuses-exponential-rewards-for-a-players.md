# QuantumLight Playbook — Performance Bonuses: Exponential Rewards for A-Players

QuantumLight/Revolut performance bonus system. Bonuses = Bonus Potential × Bonus Multiplier × Period Adjustment.

Bonus Multiplier: weighted average of individual performance (exponential based on grade) and team/company performance (based on KPI achievement %). Weights shift with seniority — individual heavier for junior, team heavier for senior.

Example: Senior engineer Alice — individual multiplier 1.75x, team KPI multiplier 1.2x, weighted 65/35 = 1.435x. Bonus potential 20%, joined Feb (11/12 stub). Final: 20% × 1.435 × 92% = 26.3% of salary.

Process: once per year. KPI quality control → calculations → calibrations (management adjustments for exceptional achievements/underperformance/conduct) → announcement & award.

Equity vs Cash bonuses:
- Equity preferred for scale-ups: preserves cash, aligns incentives. Quick vesting (50% immediate, rest over 2 years). No silver bullet equity instrument — depends on size, growth, location, regulation. Maximize tax efficiency. Hire in-house shares expert at scale.
- Cash bonuses: for consistently exceptional A-players (retention, on top of equity, measured in weeks of salary) and quota carriers (Sales, Recruiters, Support — low base, quota-based).
