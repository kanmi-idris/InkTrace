# 60fps.design: App Interaction and Motion Reference Library

Source checked: 2026-09-27.

## Relationship to existing records
- This homepage supplement expands src-2026-05-01-006, which captures the site's bottom-sheet filter and remains the detailed record for that page.

## Scope
- 60fps.design presents a reference collection of interaction details from consumer apps.
- The homepage currently lists about 2,100 shots, 492 apps, 88 app sites, 83 motion references, and 67 storyboards. These counts are site-reported and volatile.
- Navigation also exposes app references, motion, storyboards, a mockup tool, a glossary, jobs, and an MCP section.

## Research features
- Filters cover interaction and visual patterns such as blur, bottom sheets, cards, chat, drag, gesture, liquid glass, maps, morphs, onboarding, parallax, pull, spring physics, swipe, tabs, and transitions.
- The site is useful for studying motion behavior and interaction details rather than only static screen composition.
- The existing bottom-sheet record documents the site's filtered gallery and its partially gated Pro content.

## Evidence and limits
- Collection counts, labels, and access boundaries are site-reported.
- Individual shots show design examples. They do not establish platform conventions or usability outcomes by themselves.
