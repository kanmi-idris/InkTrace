# Appkittie: Mobile App Market, Ads, and Onboarding Intelligence

Source checked: 2026-09-27.

## Scope
- Appkittie is a mobile-app intelligence product for app founders, studios, and growth teams.
- The homepage describes coverage of iOS and Android store data, app revenue and download signals, advertisements, viral videos, keywords, and onboarding examples.
- The site advertises daily updates and new winning-ad discoveries.

## Product areas
- The displayed feature set includes a database of more than 4.5 million apps, a winning-ads library, a viral-videos library, a high-converting-onboardings library, ad-spend tracking, global rankings, ASO tracking, review tracking, and API credits.
- The displayed subscription snapshot is $79 per month for monthly billing or $49 per month when billed yearly.
- Both displayed plans include 5,000 API credits per month.

## Research value
- Appkittie extends visual inspiration into market and growth research.
- It can help compare competitor positioning, creative advertising, store performance signals, onboarding patterns, and app-growth tactics.

## Evidence and limits
- App counts, update frequency, customer numbers, pricing, performance signals, and conversion language are vendor-reported claims.
- Revenue and ad-spend data should be treated as estimates until independently verified.
