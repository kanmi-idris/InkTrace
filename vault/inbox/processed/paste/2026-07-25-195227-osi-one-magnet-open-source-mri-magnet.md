# OSI² ONE Magnet - Open Source MRI Magnet

https://www.opensourceimaging.org/project/osii-one-magnet/ — OSI² ONE Magnet: open-source permanent magnet for MRI of the head and extremities. Part of the Open Source Imaging initiative (OSI² e.V.).

Design: "ROMA" (Rotation Optimized Magnet) array — optimizes individual magnet orientation for maximum homogeneity (differs from standard Halbach arrays).

Specs:
- 1936× (12×12×12)mm³ NdFeBr N52 magnets
- 384× (50×12×12)mm³ NdFeBr N52 magnets
- B0 ~ 50mT
- Weight ~100kg
- Bore = ø322mm × 428+14mm (with back plate, without gradients)
- Outer dimensions: 450×520×567mm³

License: CERN OHL W v2
Estimated cost: €10,000
Repository: gitlab.com/osii/magnet/30cm-halbach-magnet

Contributors: Martin Häuer, Tom O'Reilly (LUMC), Sebastian Schachel, Wouter Teeuwisse (LUMC), Lukas Winter (PTB — Physikalisch-Technische Bundesanstalt, Berlin)

Related projects: Mini-Bumblebee, FENCE (also on opensourceimaging.org)

Publications:
1. O'Reilly et al. — In vivo 3D brain and extremity MRI at 50 mT using Halbach array. Magn.Reson.Med. 2020
2. de Vos et al. — Gradient Coil Design for Halbach-Based MRI System. IEEE Trans.Magnetics 2020
3. O'Reilly et al. — 3D MRI in homogeneous 27 cm diameter bore Halbach Array. J.Magn.Reson. 2019
