# Page Flows: Annotated Real-World UX Flow Library

Source checked: 2026-09-27.

## Scope
- Page Flows is a curated library of real-world app and website user flows.
- The homepage highlights onboarding, login, checkout, search, and other journeys from well-known digital products.

## Research features
- Screen recordings show interactions in sequence instead of presenting isolated screenshots only.
- Step-by-step annotations call out relevant UI and UX details.
- Filters support discovery by category, industry, and UX pattern.
- The site positions the library for designers, product managers, and developers who want to inspect existing journeys before designing or implementing their own.

## Access snapshot
- The displayed quarterly plan is $13 per user per month, billed at $39 per quarter after a three-day trial.
- The displayed yearly plan is $8.25 per user per month, billed at $99 per year after a three-day trial.
- A team plan is displayed at $199 per year.

## Evidence and limits
- Pricing, feature availability, customer counts, and trusted-brand claims are site snapshots and can change.
- The examples show how other products work. They do not prove that copying a flow will produce the same results.
