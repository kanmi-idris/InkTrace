# Privacy Guides — Recommended Privacy Tools

**URL:** https://www.privacyguides.org/en/tools/
**Last Updated:** March 30, 2026
**Site:** Privacy Guides (non-profit, ad-free, no affiliate links)

Comprehensive privacy tool/service recommendations organized by category.

## Browsers
- **Tor Browser** (Desktop & Android) — top choice for anonymity, access to Tor network
- **Mullvad Browser** — Tor Browser without Tor network, anti-fingerprinting for VPN users
- **Firefox** — strong privacy settings, Enhanced Tracking Protection
- **Brave** — private-by-default Chromium-based
- **Cromite** (Android) — Chromium fork with ad-blocking, successor to Bromite
- **Safari** (iOS) — anti-fingerprinting, private browsing cookie isolation
- **Extensions:** uBlock Origin, uBlock Origin Lite, AdGuard for iOS

## VPN Providers (Top 3)
- **Proton VPN** — 112+ countries, WireGuard, cash payments, partial port forwarding
- **IVPN** — 37+ countries, WireGuard, Monero & cash, no port forwarding
- **Mullvad** — 49+ countries, WireGuard, Monero & cash, IPv6 support

## Email Providers (Top 3)
- **Proton Mail** — Switzerland, 500MB–1GB free, since 2013
- **Mailbox Mail** — Germany, 2GB+ storage, eco-friendly
- **Tuta** — Germany, 1GB free, since 2011 (formerly Tutanota)
- **Aliasing:** Addy.io, SimpleLogin
- **Clients:** Thunderbird, Apple Mail, FairEmail (Android), GNOME Evolution, K-9 Mail, Mailvelope, NeoMutt

## Cloud Storage
- Proton Drive, Tresorit, Peergos

## DNS
- **Providers:** Mullvad, Quad9, others
- **Proxies:** RethinkDNS, DNSCrypt-Proxy

## Financial Services
- **Payment Masking:** Privacy.com (US), MySudo (US, paid)
- **Gift Cards:** Coincards

## Search Engines
- Brave Search, DuckDuckGo, SearXNG, Startpage
