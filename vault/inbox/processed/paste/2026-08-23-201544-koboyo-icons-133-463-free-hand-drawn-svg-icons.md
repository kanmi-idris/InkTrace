# Koboyo Icons: 133,463 Free Hand-Drawn SVG Icons

## Source overview
Koboyo Icons is a searchable library of 133,463 hand-drawn SVG icons. The site says the icons can also be used on an infinite drawing canvas.

The library supports search by concepts such as handshake, globe, roadmap, secure, and empty state. The page includes navigation for the icon library, license, feedback, drawing, GitHub, and npm.

## Library organization
The site exposes four drawing styles:
- Original: 69,383 icons.
- Cartoon: 62,227 icons.
- Solid: 1,853 icons.
- All styles: 133,463 icons.

The site groups icons into five broad categories:
- Face: 1,305.
- Mark: 11,829.
- Object: 78,858.
- People: 39,448.
- Scene: 2,023.

The visible catalogue includes icons for software concepts, business, education, accessibility, finance, devices, objects, people, scenes, and everyday items. Each icon has a dedicated page under /icons/<slug>.

## Access and use
The license page states that all 133,463 icons are free for personal and commercial use. No attribution, sign-up, seat count, or per-project fee is required.

Allowed uses include:
- Websites, apps, documents, presentations, diagrams, videos, and printed material.
- Commercial and non-commercial work.
- Recoloring, resizing, cropping, animation, and other modification.
- Products sold to customers, including client work and templates, when the icons are part of something larger.

## Restrictions
The license prohibits:
- Reselling or redistributing the library, or a substantial part of it, as an icon collection.
- Listing the collection on stock or marketplace sites.
- Building a competing icon product or icon library.
- Building a competing canvas, whiteboard, diagramming, presentation, or drawing application.
- Bundling the icons into an app where the icons are the main feature or users can pick, extract, download, or reshare them.
- Claiming authorship of the collection or registering marks based on it.

Attribution is not required, but the license says a link to https://koboyo.com/icons is appreciated. The library is provided as-is without warranty.
