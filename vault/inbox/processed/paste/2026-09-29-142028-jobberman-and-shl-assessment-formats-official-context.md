# Jobberman and SHL assessment formats: Official context

Source checked: 2026-09-29.

## Official Jobberman information checked
- Jobberman's aptitude-test article is https://www.jobberman.com/discover/aptitude-test.
- Jobberman's employer skills-assessment page is https://www.jobberman.com/employer/skills-assessments.
- The aptitude-test article describes aptitude tests as measures of thinking ability and problem-solving skills. It lists common formats including numerical reasoning, verbal reasoning, abstract or logical reasoning, cognitive and personality assessments, and mechanical or spatial reasoning.
- The article recommends timed practice and says Jobberman assessments can be taken online at home. It does not state that a particular Heirs Holdings assessment uses SHL.
- The employer page says Jobberman assessments are designed for job-role categories, can be customized, and produce section-level reports. This indicates that the exact assessment mix can vary by employer and role.

## Official SHL information checked
- SHL's practice-test page is https://www.shl.com/shldirect/en/practice-tests/.
- SHL presents both standard multiple-choice and interactive practice formats.
- SHL's numerical reasoning practice is described as using tables of statistical and numerical data and requiring logical deductions.
- These SHL formats overlap with the user's advice about ratios, percentage changes, and graph or table reading. This overlap does not prove that the Heirs Holdings assessment is an SHL assessment.

## Historical Heirs context
- A 2022 participant account published by the Tony Elumelu Foundation is https://www.tonyelumelufoundation.org/articles/how-i-got-into-the-heirs-holdings-graduate-trainee-programme.
- The account describes one historical Heirs graduate trainee aptitude test as having logical reasoning, quantitative aptitude, English proficiency, and analytical thinking.
- This is an anecdotal historical account, not a current official Heirs Holdings test specification.
