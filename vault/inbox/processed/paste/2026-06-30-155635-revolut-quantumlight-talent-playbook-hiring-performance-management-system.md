# Revolut/QuantumLight Talent Playbook — Hiring & Performance Management System

Open-sourced Revolut's talent system by QuantumLight Capital. Revolut grew from 100 employees (2017) to 12,000+ (2025), processing 1M+ applications/year.

Core philosophy: Exceptional talent > Large teams. Density scales, bureaucracy doesn't.

Three radical hiring principles:
1. Hire ambitious, junior problem solvers over "experts" — prioritize raw ambition/trajectory over legacy experience. 7-8 years for leadership, 2-3 years for ICs.
2. Build an internal recruiting machine — external recruiters don't optimize for long-term success. Internal team with quota-based comp.
3. Hyper-structure everything — consistency defeats bias. Rigidly standardized process or talent bar decays.

Solve for attitude, not tenure. Found success with individuals on fast upward trajectory early in career rather than experienced executives who won't get hands dirty.

The 3-Interview Framework (for nearly every role):
1. Problem-Solving Assessment — multi-layered case study with 2-3 layer problem, multiple root causes, quantitative elements. Candidate must ask for data to test quantitative reasoning.
2. Bar Raiser (Values & Achievements) — career progression, failures, metrics. Look for definitive proof of top performance recognized by previous managers.
3. People Management & Hiring Assessment (for managers) — case study on goal management and protecting talent bar when hiring.

5-Step Recruitment Machine Launch Sequence:
1. Hire first recruiters — one great internal recruiter = 10-15 A-players/year
2. Identify role owners — key leaders own what "good" looks like
3. Define company-wide talent bar — Problem-Solving + Bar Raiser as non-negotiable
4. Design standardized playbooks — document end-to-end hiring, certify interviewers
5. Productize your process — embed scorecards into ATS. (They built Revolut People for this.)
