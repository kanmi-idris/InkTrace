# Open Screenshot Generator: Local App Store and Play Store Graphics

## Source overview
Open Screenshot Generator is a free browser and desktop design studio for App Store and Google Play graphics. The homepage states that it is open source, runs in the browser, needs no account, and adds no watermark.

## Workflow
1. Choose a template.
2. Add app screenshots.
3. Export store-ready sizes.

The templates cover fitness, finance, podcasts, games, chat, streaming, meditation, crypto, beauty, and other categories.

## Supported outputs and devices
- App Store and Google Play screenshot graphics.
- iPhone, Android, tablets, Mac, and Apple Watch frames.
- Rotatable, scalable, and 3D-posed device frames.
- Text, shapes, arrows, blobs, speech bubbles, images, layers, and undo.
- Curated Google Fonts, including Arabic and Urdu families.
- App preview videos from screen recordings, headlines, and tap hints.
- A mode that conforms raw captures to App Store Connect video requirements.
- Video export requires WebCodecs in the browser, such as Chrome or Edge. The desktop app includes it.
- Automatic text translation into more than 50 languages.

## Privacy model
The homepage states that the editor has no backend, projects stay in browser storage, and exports render locally. It says screenshots do not leave the machine unless the user sends them to an AI provider.

The optional AI agent is the exception. It can send screenshots and a design instruction directly from the browser to Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google using the user's API key. It can also use an existing Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini session. The desktop app includes keyless local providers such as Ollama and LM Studio.

## AI agent
The agent chooses a template, places screenshots in the correct frames, and rewrites headlines from a short instruction. The homepage states that it does not draw freehand, so poor instructions can produce an odd design rather than a broken layout.

## Availability
The source code is public on GitHub. The tool supports modern browsers and desktop apps for Windows, macOS, and Linux.
