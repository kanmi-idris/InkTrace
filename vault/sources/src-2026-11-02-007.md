# Apple Help: Provide information for a complete review

## Summary
Apple says incomplete submissions are rejected for missing information and then enter a new review after resubmission. Its guidance calls for current demo credentials and useful review details. [src-2026-11-02-007]

## Key Claims
- Apple says a complete submission avoids rejection for missing information. An incomplete submission is rejected and needs a new review after resubmission. [src-2026-11-02-007]
- Apple asks for current demo-account credentials for account-based features and additional credentials in Notes when needed. [src-2026-11-02-007]
- For apps that require specific hardware, Apple asks for a video, not a screen recording, demonstrating the app on a physical device. [src-2026-11-02-007]
- Apple's video instruction is conditional, not a general requirement for every new app. [src-2026-11-02-007]

## Notes
This page does not state that a rejection will cost a fixed number of weeks. Any such wait depends on the individual queue and resubmission.
