# 9to5Mac report: Apple App Review submissions in April 2026

## Summary
A 9to5Mac report dated April 6, 2026, quotes Apple as saying the team processed over 200,000 app submissions per week during the prior 12 weeks. [src-2026-11-02-008]

## Key Claims
- Apple reportedly said over 200,000 app submissions were processed each week over the prior 12 weeks. [src-2026-11-02-008]
- Apple reportedly said 90% were processed within 48 hours, with a 1.5-day average review time. [src-2026-11-02-008]

## Notes
This is a media report of Apple's statement. It is not a dated Apple statistics page. Compare “app submissions” cautiously with Apple's App Store page wording “apps.” [src-2026-11-02-005] [src-2026-11-02-008]
