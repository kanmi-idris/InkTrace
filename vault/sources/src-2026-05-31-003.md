# Fintech app development: Process, stack & cost (2026)

## Summary
Netguru’s fintech app development guide frames fintech delivery as a compliance-first architecture problem: product category, PCI-DSS scope, PSD2/SCA, KYC/AML, GDPR residency, partner-platform selection, and post-launch monitoring shape the cost and timeline before implementation begins. It is strongest as a checklist for early fintech architecture and delivery planning, with vendor-positioning claims treated cautiously. [src-2026-05-31-003]

## Key Claims
- The guide argues that many fintech projects miss timelines because compliance scope, banking-as-a-service constraints, or KYC/AML architecture are underestimated early rather than because of ordinary coding failures. [src-2026-05-31-003]
- It maps fintech categories to different compliance and architecture burdens: neobanking and payments are high-complexity, while wealthtech, insurtech, regtech, and embedded finance vary based on custody, reporting, partner, and licensing requirements. [src-2026-05-31-003]
- PCI-DSS, PSD2 SCA, KYC/AML, GDPR data residency, SOC 2 readiness, audit logging, re-KYC, and suspicious-activity reporting are presented as architectural and operational concerns, not late-stage checkboxes. [src-2026-05-31-003]
- For payment and account-access flows, the article recommends a dedicated SCA exemption layer, FIDO2/WebAuthn-style attestation, and transaction-specific dynamic-linking payloads rather than generic app-unlock biometrics. [src-2026-05-31-003]
- The stack guidance emphasizes Node.js for event-driven payment notifications, idempotency keys for payment endpoints, cloud infrastructure aligned with audit needs, and common fintech integrations such as Plaid and Stripe Connect. [src-2026-05-31-003]
- The mobile-framework section treats React Native versus Flutter as a compliance-heavy UI tradeoff: React Native is favored for JavaScript ecosystem depth, hiring, web-sharing, and native-input behavior, while Flutter is favored for rendering consistency but carries extra scrutiny around Skia-rendered sensitive inputs. [src-2026-05-31-003]
- The article’s cost model ties budget to compliance tier, with broad 2026 ranges from $80k-$180k for a simpler MVP, $200k-$500k for mid-market builds, and $600k-$1.5M+ for enterprise or high-compliance systems. [src-2026-05-31-003]
