# Shapeshift public demo site snapshot

## Summary
The public Shapeshift demo shows a single “Type anything” input and says text can become one of 19 card types. Its accessible help text lists keyboard controls. [src-2026-10-12-019]

## Key Claims
- The page says a plan, list, color, or sum can become a card and that `/` opens all 19 card types. [src-2026-10-12-019]
- The page says Enter adds a card, Escape clears, and Tab keeps a preview. [src-2026-10-12-019]
- The page links to GitHub, displays 868 stars at capture time, and credits `anishfn`. The star count can change. [src-2026-10-12-019]
- The repository says cards are saved in localStorage until deleted; I did not test storage behavior. [src-2026-10-12-018]
