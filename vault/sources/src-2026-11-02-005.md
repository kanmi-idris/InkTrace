# Apple App Store page: weekly App Review volume

## Summary
Apple's undated App Store page says nearly 500 dedicated experts review over 130K apps every week. [src-2026-11-02-005]

## Key Claims
- Apple publishes the wording “over 130K apps” reviewed weekly by nearly 500 experts. [src-2026-11-02-005]
- The page does not date the weekly 130K measurement. [src-2026-11-02-005]
- The page says Apple rejected more than 1.9 million app submissions in 2024. [src-2026-11-02-005]

## Notes
Apple's page says “apps,” not “app submissions.” Avoid assuming this is the same unit or scope as the 200K and 378K press-reported figures. [src-2026-11-02-005] [src-2026-11-02-008] [src-2026-11-02-009]
