# ZapFast: Native unofficial WhatsApp client in Rust

## Summary
ZapFast describes itself as an unofficial Rust and egui WhatsApp companion client for Linux, macOS, and Windows. The site documents chat features, limits, account risks, and developer-published Linux performance measurements. [src-2026-10-01-008]

## Key Claims
- The site warns that unofficial-client use may violate WhatsApp's terms and may lead to account restrictions. No account was linked or tested. [src-2026-10-01-008]
- The published performance measurements cover one Linux desktop and four paired runs. They do not establish macOS or Windows performance, full synchronization time, or the incremental cost of a browser tab. [src-2026-10-01-008]
