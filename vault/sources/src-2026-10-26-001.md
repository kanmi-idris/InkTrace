# Jay Meistrich’s Legend Spark announcement on X

## Summary
Jay Meistrich announces Legend Spark, a React Native desktop framework built on Expo Desktop. He says it targets setup, Expo compatibility, and desktop modules. The post makes a speed claim but gives no measured results. [src-2026-10-26-001]

## Key Claims
- The author says Spark uses Expo Desktop and aims to support one Expo project across platforms. [src-2026-10-26-001]
- The author says Spark includes desktop modules and patches for libraries that lack desktop support. He plans to submit upstream support and maintain patches in the meantime. [src-2026-10-26-001]
- The author says new apps can run in a prebuilt runner without Xcode or a native build. Custom builds are available for new native modules or publishing. [src-2026-10-26-001]
- The author says Legend apps are built on Spark as a testbed. The post’s app list repeats Music. [src-2026-10-26-001]
- The author says Windows support is in progress and may have gaps. [src-2026-10-26-001]
