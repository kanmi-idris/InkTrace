# Job search with a 90-day notice period

## Summary
Manali Mango links a guide for technology workers in India with a 90-day notice period. The guide recommends interview preparation before resignation, then a staged job search during the notice period. It is context-specific advice, not a universal rule. [src-2026-10-06-002]

## Key Claims
- The guide recommends using the first 30 to 40 days of notice for preparation and increasing applications when 45 to 60 days remain. [src-2026-10-06-002]
- It recommends relevant applications through several channels and regular updates to the candidate's joining timeline. [src-2026-10-06-002]
- Its suggestion of 10 to 15 applications per day is the author's advice. I found no evidence in the guide to validate that volume. [src-2026-10-06-002]
