# How to Master Graph Engineering (Full Course)

## Summary

Machina (@EXM7777, 127K followers) published a full course on graph engineering via X Articles (Jul 22, 2026, 268.9K views). The course covers: what a graph is, the one pattern that pays for itself, the stop rule that protects your bill, and 3 complete builds (research desk, SEO content machine, go-to-market kit). Core framing: "a graph is a plan for your AI work, drawn out so you can see it."

## Key Claims

1. **Graph as a visible plan:** "A graph is a plan for your AI work, drawn out so you can see it." Nodes are agents/steps, edges are routing between them.

2. **The one pattern that pays for itself** — likely the parallel research + adversarial verification pattern: agents research in parallel, then try to kill each other's findings, handing you one synthesized result.

3. **The stop rule protects your bill** — explicit termination conditions on graph execution to prevent runaway costs.

4. **3 complete builds:**
   - Research desk — parallel research agents with adversarial review
   - SEO content machine — automated content pipeline across multiple agents
   - Go-to-market kit — multi-agent launch workflow

5. **Pitched as a practical, business-relevant skill** — Machina's angle is building agent graphs that produce revenue, connected to his paid community at weeklyaiops.com.
