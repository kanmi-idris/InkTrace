# Train your own Decision Model with Unsloth

## Summary
Unsloth documents training and serving LLM-based decision models. The guide reports 8 GB VRAM for Qwen3.5-2B, not Qwen3.5-4B, and gives separate 4B results on an L4. [src-2026-10-09-002]

## Key Claims
- The guide maps input and questions to structured choices or scores with probabilities. It describes 4-bit LoRA training, calibration on held-out data, and serving through the Decision API or Unsloth Studio. [src-2026-10-09-002]
- Its hardware table lists Qwen3.5-2B at 8 GB VRAM and 81% test accuracy. It does not list 8 GB for Qwen3.5-4B. [src-2026-10-09-002]
- The guide separately claims Qwen3.5-4B reached 76% in 10 minutes on an L4. These are vendor-reported results. [src-2026-10-09-002]
- The guide describes a 3,000-row test set from typed-decisions, BANKING77, and CLINC150, and says it was decontaminated against training data. This is Unsloth's statement. [src-2026-10-09-002]
- The guide says serving requires a GPU, the first request can take several minutes, and the API waits while training uses that GPU. [src-2026-10-09-002]
- The guide's introduction says “Qwen3.8” in one place, while later examples and tables use “Qwen3.5.” [src-2026-10-09-002]
