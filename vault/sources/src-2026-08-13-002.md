# CBN Regulatory Sandbox Programme: Cohort 2 Press Release

## Summary

The CBN press release announces Cohort 2 of its Regulatory Sandbox Programme. It invites eligible innovators and financial-technology organisations to apply under a VASP track or a Data-Enabled Financial Services (Non-VASP) track. [src-2026-08-13-002]

## Key Claims

- The Non-VASP track covers permission-based data sharing that can improve financial inclusion, payments, credit, risk management, operational efficiency, and consumer outcomes. [src-2026-08-13-002]
- Testing is supervised and limited to parameters agreed with the CBN. [src-2026-08-13-002]
- Sandbox participation does not grant a licence or approval to operate outside the approved test parameters. [src-2026-08-13-002]
- Applications run from 2026-08-12 through 2026-08-31. [src-2026-08-13-002]
