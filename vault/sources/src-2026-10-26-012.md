# Introducing TanStack Redact launch blog

## Summary
TanStack's launch blog announces Redact 0.2.0 as a smaller browser runtime for React apps. It publishes starter-app bundle comparisons and describes synchronous rendering as a trade-off. [src-2026-10-26-012]

## Key Claims
- The blog says Redact 0.2.0 is available for TanStack Start, Vite, Vinext, and Next.js. [src-2026-10-26-012]
- The blog reports gzip client JS and CSS savings of 47.0 KB for Start, 48.1 KB for Vite, 39.3 KB for Vinext, and 35.7 KB for Next.js. [src-2026-10-26-012]
- It says the numbers sum all emitted client JavaScript and CSS, include lazy chunks, and do not promise one page's download size. [src-2026-10-26-012]
- It reports a separate six-round Vinext benchmark at 154.9 KB with React and 115.5 KB with Redact, a 39.4 KB reduction. [src-2026-10-26-012]
- It says Redact renders synchronously without React's time slicing or interruptible renders. [src-2026-10-26-012]
- Tanner Linsley says Redact began as a smaller runtime for TanStack websites and TanStack intends to maintain it. [src-2026-10-26-012]

## Notes
The published comparisons are not independent measurements. The official docs warn that runtime bundle sizes and microbenchmarks do not establish whole-app speedups. [src-2026-10-26-013] I did not reproduce the benchmarks.
