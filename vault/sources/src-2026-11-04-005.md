# FTC CAN-SPAM compliance guide

## Summary
FTC guidance says commercial email must include a physical postal address and an opt-out method. Its guide lists penalties of up to $53,088 for each separate violating email. [src-2026-11-04-005]

## Key Claims
- CAN-SPAM applies to commercial promotional email, not only bulk email. [src-2026-11-04-005]
- Commercial messages must include a valid physical postal address and a working opt-out method. [src-2026-11-04-005]
- The FTC guide lists a maximum civil penalty of $53,088 per separate violating email. [src-2026-11-04-005]

## Notes
A waitlist launch message may qualify as commercial, depending on its primary purpose. The maximum is not an automatic charge for every email sent. [src-2026-11-04-005]
