# Sand3D token comparison method

## Summary
The page reports a 4.0× median token ratio for 30 CAD parts against LLM-written Blender Python. Its published token totals produce an aggregate ratio of 3.793×, which rounds to the post's 3.8× claim. Only 9 of 30 Blender Python outputs are marked correct. [src-2026-11-05-009]

## Interpretation
The 3.8× figure is a ratio of total token counts, not an unweighted mean of per-part ratios. The comparison is limited to the stated benchmark sample. [src-2026-11-05-009]
