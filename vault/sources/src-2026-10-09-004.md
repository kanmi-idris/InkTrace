# OpenUI generative UI benchmark and published methodology

## Summary
OpenUI's benchmark compares its own UI format with Google A2UI and Vercel json-render. The page reports render, structural-validity, cost, and production-repair results. Its methodology limits the validity metric to structural checks. OpenUI built the benchmark, and its repository publishes raw outputs and scored verdicts. [src-2026-10-09-004]

## Key Claims
- The method uses 46 interface briefs, 70 equivalent components, four generations per brief, six models, and three formats. The page reports 1,104 runs per format. [src-2026-10-09-004]
- OpenUI reports 99.9% render success and 96.9% structural validity. It reports 1.8 to 2.6 times lower cost across priced models. These are vendor benchmark results. [src-2026-10-09-004]
- OpenUI says its production sanitizer repaired 88% of failed validations. The page says this number comes from OpenUI Cloud traffic, not benchmark runs. [src-2026-10-09-004]
- The methodology says the benchmark does not measure visual quality or verify that every requirement was covered. Cost estimates use list prices and may omit hidden reasoning costs. [src-2026-10-09-004]
- A spot check of one raw output and its scored result found a renderable but structurally invalid generation, due to a `Card/align` enum mismatch. [src-2026-10-09-004]
