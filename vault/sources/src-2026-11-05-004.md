# Electronics 101 interactive learning app

## Summary
The app lists 48 lessons across seven groups and 13 language choices. The first Basics lesson includes an overview, components, a circuit diagram, and a maths panel. [src-2026-11-05-004]

## Observed Details
- Lesson groups and counts: Basics 9, Resistors 8, LEDs & diodes 5, Capacitors 9, Switches & buttons 4, Transistors 6, Sensors 7. The counts sum to 48. [src-2026-11-05-004]
- Language choices: English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Turkish, Indonesian, Ukrainian, Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), Japanese, Korean, Arabic, and Hebrew. [src-2026-11-05-004]
- The first lesson's example calculation is `I = V ÷ R = 3.0 V ÷ 10 Ω = 300 mA`. [src-2026-11-05-004]

## Caveats
The app page and language list were inspected. I did not test all lessons or translation quality. A browser click did not change the visible switch value, so circuit toggling remains unverified. [src-2026-11-05-004]
