# PlotWeaver language platform coverage

## Summary
PlotWeaver's languages page describes broader STORM-OS V3 platform coverage of 73 African languages across 44 countries. This is not the same scope as the STORM Ensemble 3.0 model's 64-language claim. [src-2026-10-31-003] [src-2026-10-31-002]

## Key Claims
- PlotWeaver says STORM-OS V3 covers 73 African languages across 44 countries. [src-2026-10-31-003]
- It says five languages have native voices, speech-to-text, and translation. [src-2026-10-31-003]
- The page lists 1.1 billion speakers across its stated platform coverage. [src-2026-10-31-003]
- These platform figures have a broader scope than the 64-language STORM Ensemble 3.0 speech synthesis model claim. [src-2026-10-31-003] [src-2026-10-31-002]
