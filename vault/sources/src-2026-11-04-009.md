# 17 U.S.C. §504: copyright statutory damages

## Summary
U.S. copyright law allows $750 to $30,000 in statutory damages per work as the court considers just. The maximum rises to $150,000 per work only if infringement is proven willful and the court so finds. [src-2026-11-04-009]

## Key Claims
- Section 504 states $750 to $30,000 per work for statutory damages, subject to the court's discretion. [src-2026-11-04-009]
- Up to $150,000 per work applies when willful infringement is proven and found by the court. [src-2026-11-04-009]
- This statutory-damage ceiling does not mean an app operator automatically owes $150,000 for every user-uploaded image. Liability and safe-harbor eligibility are separate issues. [src-2026-11-04-008] [src-2026-11-04-009]
