# Motif: Design guidance and checks for coding agents

## Summary
Motif is an MCP design-guidance service for coding agents. Its library includes implementation guidance and checks for web, app, motion, and data design. [src-2026-10-04-002]

## Key Claims
- Motif says it has 738 guides and can check measured design properties. The site says some guides are still under editorial review and may contain errors. [src-2026-10-04-002]
- The terms say full request limits vary by plan. They also say unused monthly allowances do not roll over, which conflicts with site pricing copy. [src-2026-10-04-002]
- The privacy policy says project briefs are private to each account and usage records include MCP call names, outcomes, and times. [src-2026-10-04-002]
