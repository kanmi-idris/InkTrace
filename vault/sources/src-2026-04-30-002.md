# react-native-enriched-markdown branded text selection update

## Summary
This source captures a user-supplied feature announcement for `react-native-enriched-markdown` describing new text-selection customization props available in the nightly release. The update is positioned as a design-system improvement rather than a major architecture change: it lets apps style the selection highlight and, on Android, the cursor handles to better match branded interaction surfaces. [src-2026-04-30-002]

## Key Claims
- The supplied text says `react-native-enriched-markdown` now exposes a `selectionColor` prop for customizing the text-selection highlight background. [src-2026-04-30-002]
- It also says the library exposes a `selectionHandleColor` prop for customizing selection handles on Android. [src-2026-04-30-002]
- The change is framed as useful for keeping text-selection interactions visually aligned with a broader design system. [src-2026-04-30-002]
- The feature is described as arriving first in the nightly build distributed through `react-native-enriched-markdown@nightly`. [src-2026-04-30-002]
