# Aakash Gupta X post and video: app liability claims

## Summary
The post warns that six-figure liability can arise before an app makes sales. Its video presents six legal risks involving COPPA, Google Fonts, session replay, CAN-SPAM, auto-renewals, and user-uploaded images. [src-2026-11-04-001]

## Key Claims
- The video cites maximum or statutory amounts, then frames them as flat per-event liabilities. Several amounts exist in law, but the triggering facts and legal scope vary. [src-2026-11-04-002] [src-2026-11-04-004] [src-2026-11-04-005] [src-2026-11-04-008] [src-2026-11-04-009]
- It says a general-audience app incurs COPPA liability when the first 12-year-old signs up. FTC guidance says COPPA does not require general-audience services to ask age; coverage depends on child-directed status or actual knowledge. [src-2026-11-04-002]
- It describes the Munich Google Fonts award as “$100.” The court awarded €100 damages to one visitor in one German case. [src-2026-11-04-003]
- It describes CIPA exposure as $5,000 per session. California law says $5,000 per violation or three times actual damages, whichever is greater; whether a specific session-replay implementation violates CIPA is fact-dependent. [src-2026-11-04-004]
- It describes CAN-SPAM as $53,000 per email. The FTC guide states a maximum of $53,088 per separate violating commercial email, subject to the law's requirements and facts. [src-2026-11-04-005]
- It says every non-compliant subscription renewal is a gift that must be refunded. California's gift provision concerns goods, wares, merchandise, or products. A California appellate decision says the provision gives a right to retain a received product, not a general right to refund fees. [src-2026-11-04-006] [src-2026-11-04-007]
- It says a missing $6 copyright agent makes each stolen image automatically the app operator's liability. The $6 agent designation is for service providers seeking DMCA safe-harbor protection. Copyright damages can reach $150,000 per work only for willful infringement; liability is not automatic. [src-2026-11-04-008] [src-2026-11-04-009]
