# AI Engineering from Scratch

## Summary
This source captures the public landing page for `AI Engineering from Scratch`. It is best understood as open educational infrastructure for AI engineering: a staged curriculum that tries to connect math, code, tests, and artifacts across the stack from foundational ML mechanics to agent loops and autonomous swarms. [src-2026-05-11-001]

## Key Claims
- The curriculum is positioned as a structured alternative to scattered AI learning material, with 20 phases and 416 lessons arranged as a coherent sequence. [src-2026-05-11-001]
- Its strongest pedagogical claim is that major algorithms should be built from raw math before framework abstractions are introduced. [src-2026-05-11-001]
- The concept range spans both low-level ML internals and higher-level agent patterns, including tokenization, attention, agent loops, and autonomous swarms. [src-2026-05-11-001]
- The site also emphasizes runnable, local-first artifacts across four languages rather than video-first or copy-paste learning. [src-2026-05-11-001]
