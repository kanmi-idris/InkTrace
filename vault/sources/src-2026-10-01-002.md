# Duo Studio: iPhone Duo mockup studio

## Summary
Duo Studio is an interactive mockup tool for presenting screen images on a foldable iPhone Duo. It offers device poses, hinge and scene controls, and PNG export. [src-2026-10-01-002]

## Key Claims
- The page exposes inner, optional folded, and outer display image inputs, plus device finish and hinge controls. [src-2026-10-01-002]
- The page offers 2048 px or 4096 px PNG export, with a transparent-background option. [src-2026-10-01-002]

This is a device-mockup and presentation tool, not an app-screen gallery. See the raw capture for observed controls and evidence limits.
