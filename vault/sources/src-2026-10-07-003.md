# Bachs: payments and billing platform for internet businesses

## Summary
Bachs presents itself as a payments and billing platform for digital businesses. Its site advertises checkout, subscriptions, usage billing, tax, and settlement across currencies. Product and payment claims remain unverified. [src-2026-10-07-003]

## Key Claims
- The pricing page lists 1.5% for Nigerian bank transfers, capped at ₦2,000; 2% for local cards; 5% + $0.40 for international cards; 2% or 3% for mobile money; and 1.5% for supported stablecoins. [src-2026-10-07-003]
- The homepage says 150+ currencies. The FAQ says cards support 135+ currencies. The site does not explain the difference in the inspected pages. [src-2026-10-07-003]
- The Merchant Terms name Bachs Technologies Limited and state Nigerian company registration number RC 9494536. The privacy policy says Stripe and banking partners support payment processing. I did not verify these statements. [src-2026-10-07-003]
- The inspected pages did not show a financial-services license or name settlement banks. This does not prove that none exists. [src-2026-10-07-003]
