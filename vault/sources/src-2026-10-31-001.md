# Wole Fagbohun announces STORM-Ensemble 3.0

## Summary
Wole (@wolefizzy) announces PlotWeaver's STORM-Ensemble 3.0 speech synthesis model. The post claims 64 languages, 166 voices, and 3 billion parameters. [src-2026-10-31-001]

## Key Claims
- The post says STORM-Ensemble 3.0 generates speech in 64 languages with 166 distinct speaker voices. [src-2026-10-31-001]
- It describes the model as having 3 billion parameters and as PlotWeaver's most capable multilingual speech synthesis model to date. [src-2026-10-31-001]
- It says the launch video voices are model-generated, not prerecorded samples. [src-2026-10-31-001]
- It presents media, banking, telecom, education, and developer uses as intended possibilities. These are not independent deployment evidence. [src-2026-10-31-001]
- The author says he plans to speak at the Commonwealth Business Forum in Antigua the following week. This is an announcement claim, not independently verified here. [src-2026-10-31-001]
