# SnapCN product website catalogue and pricing snapshot

## Summary
The SnapCN homepage describes a copy-paste Remotion component registry. It displays 46 free components, 51 Pro components, and an early-bird Pro price banner. [src-2026-10-12-014]

## Key Claims
- The homepage lists 46 free components and gives category counts of 14 Text & Titles, 2 Captions, 5 Logos, 6 Screens & Devices, 2 Social Proof, 11 Scenes, and 6 AI Chat Input. These counts sum to 46. [src-2026-10-12-014]
- The footer says 46 components are MIT licensed and lists 51 Pro components. [src-2026-10-12-014]
- The homepage banner displayed early-bird Pro pricing through October 20: $129 per year or $249 once. This is a time-sensitive page statement, not a checkout confirmation. [src-2026-10-12-014]
- The website's 46 free-component count and 51 Pro count differ from the GitHub README's list of 21 components. The inspected pages do not explain whether the counts cover different sets or versions. [src-2026-10-12-013] [src-2026-10-12-014]
- The website says source is installed with the shadcn CLI, lands in `components/snap-cn/`, and has no SnapCN runtime. These are publisher claims; I did not test installation. [src-2026-10-12-014]
