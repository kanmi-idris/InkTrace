# Unsloth Qwen3.5-4B Decision-model notebook

## Summary
The notebook fine-tunes Qwen3.5-4B as a structured decision model. It uses 4-bit loading, LoRA, and a decision head. The notebook instructs users to run on a free Colab T4. It does not verify the post's claim about Qwen3.5-4B on 8 GB VRAM locally. [src-2026-10-09-001]

## Key Claims
- The data format uses `state`, `questions`, and `gold`, with choice, yes/no, and score question types. The notebook holds out 80 rows, reported as 400 decisions. [src-2026-10-09-001]
- The notebook reports 75–78% test accuracy after 60 steps, with about two hours on a free T4. It reports 78% after a 70-step full run on an A100 in 25 minutes. These are Unsloth's results. [src-2026-10-09-001]
- The code loads Qwen3.5-4B with 4-bit quantization, but the notebook does not report an 8 GB peak-memory result. [src-2026-10-09-001]
- The notebook says it saves the LoRA adapters, decision head, and calibration. It shows inference, but it does not run a deployment. [src-2026-10-09-001]
- The notebook states that it is licensed under LGPL-3.0. [src-2026-10-09-001]
