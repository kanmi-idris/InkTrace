# Mayron v. Google: automatic-renewal gift provision

## Summary
The California Court of Appeal read §17603 as giving consumers a right to retain products received, not as a general right to refund fees. [src-2026-11-04-007]

## Key Claims
- The court described the unconditional-gift provision as a right to keep a product already received. [src-2026-11-04-007]
- It rejected the argument that this provision itself created a right to pursue a refund of fees paid. [src-2026-11-04-007]

## Notes
This 2020 case predates California's 2025 law amendments. It supports a narrow distinction and does not resolve every current subscription or consumer-protection claim. [src-2026-11-04-006] [src-2026-11-04-007]
