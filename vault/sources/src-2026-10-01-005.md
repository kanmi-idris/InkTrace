# Live Activities with Expo, End to End: Client and Backend

## Summary
Wahab Balogun's Expo article shows an end-to-end Live Activity for server-side receipt processing. It covers a shared widget state schema, Lock Screen and Dynamic Island regions, token registration, backend mapping, and APNs updates. [src-2026-10-01-005]

## Key Claims
- The article uses a TSX widget with `@expo/ui/swift-ui` and `expo-widgets`, which compiles to SwiftUI. [src-2026-10-01-005]
- Push-to-start requires iOS 17.2 or later in the example. The client registers per-activity tokens, and the backend maps job states to the widget schema before sending APNs updates. [src-2026-10-01-005]
