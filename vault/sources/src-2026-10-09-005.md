# YouTube Playbook for Technical Creators

## Summary
The page offers a production and growth playbook for technical YouTube creators. It recommends planning packaging before recording, structuring early retention, and improving editing and review routines. It says the advice comes from five strategy and editing calls with a developer-education channel and a packaging masterclass by Theo. This is a third-party synthesis, not official YouTube guidance. [src-2026-10-09-005]

## Key Claims
- For growth videos aimed at new viewers, the page recommends a clear title and thumbnail, a scripted 15–20 second opening, early value, and re-engagements near 1, 3, and 6 minutes. It says deep dives for existing viewers can use a different structure. [src-2026-10-09-005]
- The page proposes one long-form video per week and a 4–6 month strategy test, while noting that production effort can limit cadence. These are recommendations, not guaranteed growth results. [src-2026-10-09-005]
- The page recommends a three-pass edit: structure, flow, then polish. It says the creator retains final approval while the editor owns execution and craft. [src-2026-10-09-005]
- The page presents YouTube recommendation-system details as creator advice. It says satisfaction signals are not public and labels its explanation as a working guess. Do not treat its algorithm claims as official policy. [src-2026-10-09-005]
- The page says its 1,000–10,000 daily-impression range for meaningful A/B testing is a rough VidIQ estimate, not a statistical threshold. [src-2026-10-09-005]
