# BrewKeg: NGN-priced AI inference gateway

## Summary
BrewKeg presents itself as a token-billed gateway for OpenAI- and Anthropic-format model requests. It lists Claude, GPT, and Gemini models, with NGN plans and PAYGo. [src-2026-10-03-004]

## Key Claims
- The privacy policy says full request and response content is stored by default. This includes prompts, messages, tool definitions, model responses, reasoning, and tool calls. The policy says logging can be disabled, but I could not inspect the setting without signing in. [src-2026-10-03-004]
- The pricing pages conflict on whether unused plan credit rolls over. Confirm the terms before payment. Prices and provider behavior are company claims; I did not test the service. [src-2026-10-03-004]
