# Interfaces

## Summary
This source captures the public `interfaces.dev` landing page. It is best understood as design-engineering reference infrastructure: a paid magazine that packages interface-craft knowledge into recurring issues, interactive demos, source code, curated tools, and even agent-skill artifacts rather than limiting itself to plain editorial text. [src-2026-05-13-004]

## Key Claims
- Interfaces positions itself as a design-engineering magazine focused on the details that make interfaces feel natural, intuitive, and high quality. [src-2026-05-13-004]
- The visible topic range spans animation, typography, layout, color, and adjacent interface-building concerns. [src-2026-05-13-004]
- The site is notable because it pairs editorial issues with interactive demos and source code, which makes it more implementation-oriented than a typical design newsletter. [src-2026-05-13-004]
- A distinctive extra layer is the inclusion of agent skills, suggesting that some of the magazine’s ideas are also being packaged into reusable AI-assistant guidance. [src-2026-05-13-004]
- The delivery model is ongoing rather than static: the public page says at least one new issue is released every month. [src-2026-05-13-004]
