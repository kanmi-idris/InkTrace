# California Automatic Renewal Law

## Summary
California law requires clear disclosure and affirmative consent for covered automatic-renewal offers. Its unconditional-gift provision concerns goods or products sent without consent. [src-2026-11-04-006]

## Key Claims
- Covered renewal terms must be disclosed clearly before the agreement and must receive affirmative consent. [src-2026-11-04-006]
- Terms include recurring charges, cancellation policy, and renewal or continuous-service duration. [src-2026-11-04-006]
- The gift provision applies to “goods, wares, merchandise, or products” sent without the required consent. The statute does not say every subscription renewal payment automatically becomes a gift requiring a refund. [src-2026-11-04-006]

The video overstates the statute when it presents the gift provision as a general automatic refund rule for every non-compliant software renewal. See [src-2026-11-04-007].
