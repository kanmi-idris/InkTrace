# Phonon refiner 1.7B model card

## Summary
The card reports 76,960 rows, split evenly between DeepSeek-labeled writing-mode rows and the earlier 0.2.3 refiner corpus. It also reports a 90-to-23 model-judge preference result on 119 scored lecture segments. These are project disclosures and not independent evaluations. [src-2026-11-06-004]

## Evaluation clarification
The 580-row and 606-row tables use Wispr Flow outputs as references, but they compare the new refiner with the 0.2.3 refiner. The confidence intervals include zero. They do not show direct equivalence to Wispr Flow. [src-2026-11-06-004]
