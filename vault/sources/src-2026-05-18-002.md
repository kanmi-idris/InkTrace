# Free Faces

## Summary
This source captures the public Free Faces gallery. It is best understood as a typography-discovery and licensing-reference utility: a curated collection of free-licence typefaces with category browsing, specimen previews, per-font metadata, and license visibility that makes it useful for both UI design exploration and practical font selection. [src-2026-05-18-002]

## Key Claims
- The site frames itself as a curated collection of typefaces available under a variety of free licences rather than as a single foundry or one-license library. [src-2026-05-18-002]
- It is organized for browsing across familiar design categories such as serif, sans serif, monospace, display, cursive, and slab. [src-2026-05-18-002]
- Typeface pages expose useful selection metadata including category, style count, preview specimen, glyph coverage, and explicit license labels where available. [src-2026-05-18-002]
- The gallery is therefore valuable not only for visual inspiration but also for reducing friction around practical font evaluation and licensing checks. [src-2026-05-18-002]
