# Framer AEO Scanner

## Summary
This source captures the supplied product copy and official public page for Framer’s AEO Scanner. Its main value is as a site-audit and discoverability tool: instead of focusing on classic search ranking alone, it assesses whether a page is legible and quotable to AI answer engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini. [src-2026-05-01-005]

## Key Claims
- Framer presents the scanner as a way to test whether AI systems can meaningfully find and interpret a given website URL. [src-2026-05-01-005]
- The page defines AEO, or Answer Engine Optimization, as making content easier for AI systems to find, understand, and quote. [src-2026-05-01-005]
- Framer’s framing is that being invisible to AI-generated answers can increasingly mean being invisible to the audience, because many user journeys now end inside answer interfaces rather than traditional click-through search. [src-2026-05-01-005]
- The page explicitly emphasizes metadata quality, content structure, and trust signals as factors that influence whether AI systems can effectively use a site. [src-2026-05-01-005]
- The scanner is described as assessing a single URL and using AI, with a clear caveat that results are approximate and reflect Framer’s own methodology rather than a universal standard. [src-2026-05-01-005]
