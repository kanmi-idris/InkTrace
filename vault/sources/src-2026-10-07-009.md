# OpenDocRouter: unified document-parsing API

## Summary
OpenDocRouter offers a page-based document-parsing API with selectable models. Its pricing page includes ParseBench scores and cost estimates. The product's data-retention claims need careful review. [src-2026-10-07-009]

## Key Claims
- The docs say uncached sync requests do not store documents. Cached results are encrypted for 24 hours. The linked general LlamaIndex privacy notice also says Customer Content may be used to improve services; the scope is unclear. [src-2026-10-07-009]
- The docs list a $25 minimum top-up and a 5% top-up fee. Successful pages are token-billed; failed pages are free. [src-2026-10-07-009]
- The Models page estimates GPT-6 Luna at $0.80 per 1,000 pages and Claude Opus 5.5 at $48.82 per 1,000 pages. Actual documents may cost more or less. [src-2026-10-07-009]
- OpenDocRouter uses ParseBench for model scores. ParseBench identifies itself as a LlamaIndex project, so these scores are not independent validation. [src-2026-10-07-009]
- Sync requests support up to 50 pages. Async requests support up to 500 pages and require caching. [src-2026-10-07-009]
