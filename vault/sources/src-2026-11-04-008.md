# U.S. Copyright Office DMCA designated-agent FAQ

## Summary
The Copyright Office charges $6 for a DMCA designated-agent designation, amendment, or resubmission. The designation is relevant to service providers seeking §512 safe-harbor protections. [src-2026-11-04-008]

## Key Claims
- Current designated-agent registration and update fee: $6. [src-2026-11-04-008]
- A service provider seeking the §512 safe harbors must designate an agent and keep the designation current. [src-2026-11-04-008]
- Losing safe harbor can leave a service provider exposed to certain copyright claims. It does not make every app automatically liable for every user image. [src-2026-11-04-008]

## Notes
Whether a particular app is a qualifying service provider and satisfies §512 depends on its role and compliance with all statutory conditions. [src-2026-11-04-008]
