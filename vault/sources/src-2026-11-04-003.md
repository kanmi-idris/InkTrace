# Munich Regional Court Google Fonts decision

## Summary
In a 2022 case, Landgericht München I awarded one website visitor €100 after the site disclosed the visitor's dynamic IP address to Google through remotely loaded Google Fonts without consent. [src-2026-11-04-003]

## Key Claims
- The court found unauthorized IP-address disclosure to Google in the case before it. [src-2026-11-04-003]
- The judgment ordered €100 in damages to the plaintiff, plus interest. The amount was euros, not U.S. dollars. [src-2026-11-04-003]
- This was one German first-instance case, not a universal fine amount for every site using Google Fonts. [src-2026-11-04-003]

## Notes
The court noted that Google Fonts could be hosted locally without a connection to Google's server. [src-2026-11-04-003]
