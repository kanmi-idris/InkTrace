# No AI Slop — AI Writing Slop Detection & Removal Skill

## Summary
A skill by petergyang that removes 20+ patterns of AI-generated writing slop (binary contrasts, throat-clearing openers, faux-insight setups, weasel attribution, etc.) and can detect whether a piece reads as AI. MIT license, 1.1K stars, 90 forks. Includes SKILL.md (editing rules) and eval.md (pass/fail checks). Installable in Claude Code, Codex, or any AI harness.

## Key Claims
- Removes 20+ patterns of AI slop from any piece of writing. [src-2026-07-22-008]
- Detects patterns including: binary contrasts, throat-clearing openers, faux-insight setups, colon reveals, superficial analysis, importance puffery, weasel attribution, fake-strong verbs, synonym cycling, negative listing, dramatic fragmentation. [src-2026-07-22-008]
- Enforces fundamentals: lead with the point, active voice, untangle hard-to-follow sentences, prefer concrete numbers over abstractions. [src-2026-07-22-008]
- Two modes: edit a draft (returns edited draft + "What changed") and detect slop (returns every pattern found with quoted lines). [src-2026-07-22-008]
- Makes minimum effective edit, then checks its own work against eval.md. [src-2026-07-22-008]
- Installable by pasting in Claude Code, Codex, or any AI harness. [src-2026-07-22-008]
