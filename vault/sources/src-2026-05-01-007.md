# CTA.gallery

## Summary
This source captures the public CTA.gallery page, a curated gallery of call-to-action design examples. Its main value is as conversion-oriented reference material: it gives designers and builders a concentrated set of CTA patterns, layouts, and category-specific examples drawn from live websites. [src-2026-05-01-007]

## Key Claims
- CTA.gallery positions itself as a source of call-to-action design inspiration aimed at creativity and conversion. [src-2026-05-01-007]
- The visible filters suggest the gallery organizes CTAs by interaction or business purpose, such as buttons, downloads, forms, pop-ups, navigation, newsletters, and pricing/subscription flows. [src-2026-05-01-007]
- The page explicitly includes CTA-writing tips in addition to visual examples, implying a blend of copy and design inspiration. [src-2026-05-01-007]
- The gallery is framed as a broad sampling “from across the web,” with screenshots attributed to their original owners. [src-2026-05-01-007]
