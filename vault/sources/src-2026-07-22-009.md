# Use My /No-AI-Slop Skill — Peter Yang's Announcement & Writing Philosophy

## Summary
Peter Yang's Substack post announcing his open-source /no-ai-slop skill (src-2026-07-22-008). Describes three usage modes (edit, detect, satirize), lists 10 slop patterns, introduces his 25/50/25 rule for editing with AI (human first draft → AI polish → human final pass), and argues that slop hurts trust with people whose judgment matters.

## Key Claims
- /no-ai-slop skill has three modes: edit (removes slop + lists changes), detect (lists slop patterns found), and satirize (generates cringe slop). [src-2026-07-22-009]
- 10 slop patterns: binary contrasts, throat-clearing openers, faux-insight setups, colon reveals, dramatic fragments, superficial analysis, importance puffery, weasel attribution, synonym cycling, fake-profound endings. [src-2026-07-22-009]
- 25/50/50 rule: human writes first 25% without AI, AI helps edit middle 50%, human manually edits last 25%. [src-2026-07-22-009]
- "People who know AI can see the slop coming from a mile away." [src-2026-07-22-009]
- Some creators publish 10 AI slop posts per hour and drive real results. [src-2026-07-22-009]
- Brian Chesky's hacked account posted AI thread; readers spotted it as AI before confirmation. [src-2026-07-22-009]
- Alex Cohen letter: pumping out slop puts an unfair burden on the reader. [src-2026-07-22-009]
