# Searchata pricing page

## Summary
At capture, Searchata listed a $12 monthly plan and a $79 yearly plan. It offered a seven-day trial that requires a card and renews into the selected plan unless cancelled. [src-2026-11-01-013]

## Key Claims
- Monthly price: $12 USD. Yearly price: $79 USD. [src-2026-11-01-013]
- The seven-day trial requires a card and selected plan; the plan renews automatically unless cancelled. [src-2026-11-01-013]
- Searchata says plans include GSC and Bing data, supported agent connections, and no monthly MCP-call cap for normal interactive use, subject to other safeguards and provider limits. [src-2026-11-01-013]
- An AI assistant account is not included. [src-2026-11-01-013]

## Notes
Prices can change. No trial or purchase was started.
