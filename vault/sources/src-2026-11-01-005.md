# Arc official documentation on USDC gas and contract address

## Summary
Arc's official documentation says USDC is the native EVM asset used to pay gas fees. It lists the Arc mainnet ERC-20 USDC interface and its six-decimal precision. [src-2026-11-01-005]

## Key Claims
- Arc uses USDC as its native gas asset. [src-2026-11-01-005]
- The ERC-20 interface is `0x3600000000000000000000000000000000000000` and uses six decimals. [src-2026-11-01-005]
- Native gas balance uses 18 decimals; the ERC-20 interface uses six decimals, and both share the underlying balance. [src-2026-11-01-005]

## Notes
These network facts do not verify specific Ooyaa gas payments or tips. See [src-2026-11-01-003].
