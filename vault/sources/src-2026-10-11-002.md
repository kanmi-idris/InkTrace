# ManimGX benchmark methodology and speed results

## Summary
The project benchmark reports a 90.6× speed ratio over ManimCE for five scenes on an Apple M4 Pro. The total is based on different run counts across engines and their own renderers. Treat it as a project-specific result, not a universal speedup. [src-2026-10-11-002]

## Key Claims
- The benchmark reports 3.078 seconds for ManimGX and 278.826 seconds for ManimCE across 36.2 seconds of 1080p60 output. It reports a ratio of 90.6×. [src-2026-10-11-002]
- The project says the scenes ran on an Apple M4 Pro with 12 CPU cores and 24 GB memory, using macOS 15.7.9 and ManimGX 0.1.0, ManimGL 1.7.2, ManimCE 0.21.0, and Blender 5.2.2 LTS. [src-2026-10-11-002]
- The method uses five scenes and each engine's own renderer/exporter. ManimGX and ManimGL scene times are means of three runs. ManimCE and Blender use one run per scene. [src-2026-10-11-002]
- The project says timing includes launch through finished MP4. ManimGX timing also includes storyboards and layout checks. Runs were sequential, and system/GPU caches were not cleared. [src-2026-10-11-002]
- The results come from a benchmark built and published by the ManimGX project. I did not rerun it or inspect the raw results. [src-2026-10-11-002]
