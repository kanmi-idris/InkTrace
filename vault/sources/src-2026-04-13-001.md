# MiniMax Audio

## Summary
This source captures MiniMax Audio's public product positioning from an official crawlable text-to-speech page variant plus the supplied product copy. The combined evidence presents MiniMax as a broader AI audio-creation platform for speech generation, voice customization, voice cloning, and music-oriented workflows rather than only a narrow TTS utility. [src-2026-04-13-001]

## Key Claims
- MiniMax Audio describes its TTS offering as free text-to-speech with natural AI voices for use cases such as audiobooks, video voiceovers, games, e-learning, and accessibility. [src-2026-04-13-001]
- The official page highlights free generation attempts, multilingual support, emotion or voice switching, downloadable audio, and a voice library with 300+ voices across 50+ languages and accents. [src-2026-04-13-001]
- Related official MiniMax Audio pages also position the product as an AI voice generator and expose voice-cloning capabilities that can build a custom voice from roughly 10 seconds of audio. [src-2026-04-13-001]
- The supplied product copy extends that scope to music creation and project-oriented audio workflows such as storytelling, commercial generation, and AI tutor use cases. [src-2026-04-13-001]
- The supplied copy also presents the product as a voice-browsing surface with curated presets across narration, whisper, terror, character, education, presentation, robot, and cyberpunk styles, plus music genres such as electronic, R&B, pop, jazz, country, and blues. [src-2026-04-13-001]
