# PlotWeaver STORM Ensemble 3.0 product page

## Summary
PlotWeaver's product page lists STORM Ensemble 3.0 at 64 languages, 166 named voices, 3B parameters, and 24 kHz audio. [src-2026-10-31-002]

## Key Claims
- PlotWeaver lists 64 languages and 166 named voices for STORM Ensemble 3.0. [src-2026-10-31-002]
- PlotWeaver lists 3B parameters and 24 kHz audio. [src-2026-10-31-002]
- The page says each sample voice is generated by the model and says language voices were evaluated before release. [src-2026-10-31-002]
- The page provides sixteen named voice examples across languages including Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, Nigerian Pidgin, Swahili, Zulu, Arabic, Akan, Somali, Wolof, Malagasy, Tswana, Ewe, Afrikaans, Shona, and Xhosa. [src-2026-10-31-002]
