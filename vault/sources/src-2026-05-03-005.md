# Voice-Pro

## Summary
This source captures the public `voice-pro` repository page. It is best understood as a creator-focused speech and dubbing workbench: a Gradio application that combines ASR, translation, subtitle workflows, voice separation, text-to-speech, and zero-shot voice cloning into one open-source interface. [src-2026-05-03-005]

## Key Claims
- The repository positions Voice-Pro as an all-in-one web app for speech recognition, translation, and multilingual dubbing rather than as a single-model demo. [src-2026-05-03-005]
- The stack combines multiple ASR, TTS, and cloning systems, including Whisper-family recognizers, Edge-TTS, kokoro, F5-TTS, E2-TTS, and CosyVoice. [src-2026-05-03-005]
- The app also integrates adjacent media-processing steps such as YouTube download, audio extraction, and Demucs-based voice separation. [src-2026-05-03-005]
- The UI is organized into multiple workflow tabs, including Dubbing Studio, subtitle-focused recognition, translation, and speech generation. [src-2026-05-03-005]
- The project’s maintenance note matters: the page says active development is paused while the team focuses elsewhere, even though the codebase has been opened and made free. [src-2026-05-03-005]
