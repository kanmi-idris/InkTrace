# FTC COPPA FAQ: coverage and penalties

## Summary
FTC guidance says COPPA covers child-directed services and general-audience services with actual knowledge of collecting personal information from children under 13. General-audience services are not required to ask every visitor's age. [src-2026-11-04-002]

## Key Claims
- The FTC says the Rule does not require general-audience services to ask visitors' ages. [src-2026-11-04-002]
- COPPA can apply to a general-audience service when it has actual knowledge that it collects, uses, or discloses personal information from a child under 13. Child-directed services have a separate coverage basis. [src-2026-11-04-002]
- FTC FAQ lists up to $53,088 per violation as a civil penalty ceiling. The actual amount depends on case-specific factors. [src-2026-11-04-002]

## Notes
A child account alone does not establish an automatic $53,088 charge. The service, data collected, knowledge, and enforcement facts matter. [src-2026-11-04-002]
