# Mobilegamer.biz report: App Review delays and 378K submissions per week

## Summary
A Mobilegamer.biz report dated October 20, 2026, says Apple reported 378,000 app submissions per week over the prior 12 weeks, a 1.5-day average for all apps, and 14 hours for minor updates. It also reports developer anecdotes about waits of several weeks. [src-2026-11-02-009]

## Key Claims
- Apple reportedly said the prior 12-week average was 378,000 app submissions per week. [src-2026-11-02-009]
- Apple reportedly said average review time was 1.5 days for all apps and 14 hours for minor updates. [src-2026-11-02-009]
- Multiple anonymous developers reported review waits of several weeks; these accounts are anecdotal, not a representative sample. [src-2026-11-02-009]
- The report says the slowdown appeared more pronounced for new app submissions, while one studio reported no change for updates to an established app. [src-2026-11-02-009]

## Notes
The report uses “app submissions,” while Apple's App Store page uses “apps.” The ratio 378K / 130K is about 2.9, but the sources do not establish identical units or workload scope. Do not state that the same metric tripled. [src-2026-11-02-005] [src-2026-11-02-009]

No public cohort statistic was found for first-time developers. Do not present that cohort claim as established fact.
