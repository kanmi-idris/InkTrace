# Searchata security documentation and privacy policy

## Summary
Searchata documents read-only source scopes and says it stores encrypted OAuth credentials, connected-property metadata, authorization records, and short-lived encrypted response caches. Its privacy policy also describes website analytics and session replay. [src-2026-11-01-012]

## Key Claims
- Searchata lists Google `webmasters.readonly` and Bing `webmaster.read` scopes. [src-2026-11-01-012]
- It says it stores encrypted source credentials and short-lived encrypted source-response caches. This qualifies the homepage's “We don't store your search data” wording. [src-2026-11-01-012] [src-2026-11-01-010]
- The privacy policy gives different cache lifetimes by source/report type, up to 24 hours for some Google performance caches. [src-2026-11-01-012]
- It says MCP tool-call metadata is stored up to 30 days and daily timing/count summaries up to 400 days. [src-2026-11-01-012]
- It describes X conversion measurement, Clarity, PostHog, and session replay for website use. It says GSC/Bing data and MCP results are not sent to X or Microsoft Clarity. [src-2026-11-01-012]
- Searchata documents OAuth 2.0 with PKCE and separate agent/source revocation. [src-2026-11-01-012]
