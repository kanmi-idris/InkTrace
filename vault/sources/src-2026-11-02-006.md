# Apple Developer App Review page

## Summary
Apple says at least 50% of submissions are typically reviewed in less than 24 hours, and 90% in less than 48 hours. It warns that incomplete information may delay or prevent approval. [src-2026-11-02-006]

## Key Claims
- Apple describes its review scope as apps, app updates, bundles, in-app purchases, and in-app events. [src-2026-11-02-006]
- Apple says at least 50% of submissions are typically reviewed in less than 24 hours and 90% in less than 48 hours. [src-2026-11-02-006]
- For sign-in features, Apple asks for valid demo credentials. It asks developers to provide special configuration details. [src-2026-11-02-006]
- Apple says to prepare a demo video or hardware when an app requires an environment that is hard to replicate or specific hardware. [src-2026-11-02-006]
- Apple says over 40% of unresolved issues relate to Guideline 2.1, App Completeness. [src-2026-11-02-006]

## Notes
These published targets are not an individual wait-time guarantee. The page does not provide a separate time distribution for first app submissions. [src-2026-11-02-006]
