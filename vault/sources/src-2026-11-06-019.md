# AI-Built App Legal-Risk Checklist

## Summary
A social checklist of six legal-risk traps in AI-built apps with per-incident statutory exposure and a not-legal-advice disclaimer, ending with a prompt template for Claude.

## Key Claims
- COPPA age-gating: up to $53K per child under 13. COPPA does not require all general-audience apps to collect age; enforceable per violation. [src-2026-11-06-019] [src-2026-11-04-001]
- Self-hosted fonts: a Munich court fined one site EUR 100 for one visitor's IP leak under GDPR. The award was EUR 100 in one case, not a general hack. [src-2026-11-06-019] [src-2026-11-04-001]
- Session replay on by default can count as CIPA wiretapping in California; the $5K per-session framing is one formulation, and penalties are conditional, not automatic. [src-2026-11-06-019] [src-2026-11-04-001]
- CAN-SPAM marketing email without unsubscribe or postal address: up to $53K per email. [src-2026-11-06-019]
- California auto-renewals can count as a gift the user may have to refund when renewal terms sit apart from the checkout button. The gift rule applies per case, not universally. [src-2026-11-06-019] [src-2026-11-04-001]
- Missing DMCA-agent registration costs safe harbor for user uploads; statutory damages up to $150K per work require willfulness conditions. [src-2026-11-06-019] [src-2026-11-04-001]
- All amounts are conditional statutory maxima, not automatic fines; the checklist itself ends with a not-legal-advice disclaimer. [src-2026-11-06-019]
