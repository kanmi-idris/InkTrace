# Santiago on X: Searching 32 million records in 30 milliseconds

## Summary
Santiago's sponsored X post promotes Typesense and claims a 32-million-record search takes 30 milliseconds. The live demo confirms a 32-million-song dataset, but the exact latency claim is not verified by the official benchmark page inspected. [src-2026-10-08-003]

## Key Claims
- The post is dated June 20, 2025, and explicitly says Typesense sponsored it. It lists fuzzy, vector, semantic, and geo search. [src-2026-10-08-003]
- The linked live demo uses 32 million MusicBrainz songs. Its About panel describes a geo-distributed three-node Typesense Cloud cluster in Oregon, Frankfurt, and Mumbai. The demo does not state a 30 ms latency result. [src-2026-10-08-003]
- The official benchmark page instead reports 28 ms average processing time for 28 million books at 46 QPS on a 4-vCPU server. These are different data and workload conditions. [src-2026-10-08-003]
- The X page has a 2-minute, 38-second video. I did not play or analyze it. [src-2026-10-08-003]
- The user's pasted copy adds a natural-language-search example that is absent from the X post inspected. Current Typesense documentation confirms the feature, with LLM-based query parsing and API-key setup. [src-2026-10-08-004]
