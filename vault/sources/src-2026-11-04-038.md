# Tinycharts product page and gallery

## Summary
Tinycharts presents 28 interactive chart examples and offers a free Claude skill. The page says every chart has a statechart behind it. This is a product claim; the gated skill source was not inspected. [src-2026-11-04-038]

## Key Claims
- The free skill includes 9 chart types. Pro includes all 28 and is listed at $34.90. [src-2026-11-04-038]
- The page limits free use to personal, non-commercial projects. Pro includes commercial use and 28 editable templates. The page says sales are final. [src-2026-11-04-038]
- The free skill download is email-gated. The page says the email will be used for updates. [src-2026-11-04-038]

## Caveats
The user's post says “get the free skill,” but no skill file was downloaded. The page asked for an email, and no address was submitted. The direct install command was not run. [src-2026-11-04-038]

The page credits `statechart-art` and a statechart skill. Those credits do not prove Tinycharts uses TLA+ or TLC. [src-2026-11-04-038] [src-2026-11-04-039] [src-2026-11-04-040]
