# The Register report: long App Store review waits

## Summary
The Register reported individual developer accounts of multi-week waits. One forum post said an app had been waiting since September 19; the article was published October 22, a 33-day interval. [src-2026-11-02-010]

## Key Claims
- The Register linked to developer forum threads with waits of about three weeks and cited one app waiting since September 19. [src-2026-11-02-010]
- It also cited a Reddit post titled “My app has been waiting for App Store review for almost a month.” [src-2026-11-02-010]
- These reports support that individual waits can approach or exceed a month, but they do not measure prevalence or establish a first-time-app cohort effect. [src-2026-11-02-010]

## Notes
These are anecdotal, user-submitted reports. The article also repeats Apple's general review-time targets. They do not replace a representative dataset or a breakdown by submission type. [src-2026-11-02-006] [src-2026-11-02-010]
