# BoltAI and PDF Pals Mac product pages

## Summary
BoltAI describes itself as a native Mac app built with SwiftUI and AppKit. PDF Pals describes itself as a native AI PDF assistant for Mac. [src-2026-11-01-009]

## Key Claims
- BoltAI lists macOS 13 or later and Intel and Apple Silicon support. [src-2026-11-01-009]
- PDF Pals lists macOS 13 or later, with version 2 requiring macOS 14 or later. [src-2026-11-01-009]
- These product pages document Mac support. They do not verify the completeness of the reported Windows ports. [src-2026-11-01-009] [src-2026-11-01-006]
