# California CIPA provisions and session replay

## Summary
California Penal Code §637.2 provides statutory damages of $5,000 per violation or three times actual damages, whichever is greater. Session-replay claims under CIPA §631 are fact-dependent; the statute does not set a blanket $5,000-per-session fee. [src-2026-11-04-004]

## Key Claims
- Section 631 concerns interception or reading of communications without required consent. [src-2026-11-04-004]
- Section 637.2 states $5,000 per violation or three times actual damages, whichever is greater. [src-2026-11-04-004]
- Courts have treated website-tracking claims differently depending on the statute and facts. A session-replay implementation is not automatically a violation. [src-2026-11-04-004]
- California SB 690 limits private claims under §638.51 for certain website and app pen-register claims. Section 631 remains a separate wiretap theory. [src-2026-11-04-004]

## Notes
The statute says “per violation,” not “per session.” Whether a given session-replay setup violates CIPA requires legal analysis of the specific data and operation. [src-2026-11-04-004]
