# react-native-tgfx performance documentation

## Summary
The project reports a comparison with react-native-skia 3.8.1 on one Samsung SM-E146B, using release builds and the mean of two runs. [src-2026-11-04-012]

## Key Claims
- The test suite covers shapes, images, a shader, a pattern, paths, and text. [src-2026-11-04-012]
- The project reports higher Present FPS for tgfx in each listed scene, with the largest displayed gap in the paths test. [src-2026-11-04-012]
- The prewarm section reports first-canvas time dropping from 28.4 ms to 11.9 ms, averaged across four cold starts per arm on that device. [src-2026-11-04-012]

## Notes
These are maintainer-reported measurements from one device and two benchmark runs. They do not establish general performance across devices or workloads. [src-2026-11-04-012]
