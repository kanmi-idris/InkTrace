# Greenspek Short Code and Long Code Pricing

## Summary
This source captures a Nigerian provider pricing page for bulk SMS, shortcode services, long-code SIM hosting, OTP SMS, and voice OTP. Its main value is evidentiary: it provides concrete local numbers for what different OTP and inbound-SMS infrastructure options can cost, rather than discussing reverse OTP or shortcode models only in the abstract. [src-2026-05-04-002]

## Key Claims
- The page gives a concrete sender-paid OTP baseline by listing OTP SMS at `3.3` SMS units or about `₦7.49`, alongside voice OTP at `2.8` units or about `₦6.36`. [src-2026-05-04-002]
- It presents shortcode SMS as a premium-billed channel where the user can be charged higher amounts such as `₦30`, `₦50`, or `₦100`, with carrier-specific payout examples to the service operator. [src-2026-05-04-002]
- It also provides concrete access pricing for shortcode infrastructure itself, including `₦500,000` yearly or `₦250,000` six-month shared shortcode access. [src-2026-05-04-002]
- The page further describes long-code or SIM-hosting products as another integration route, with shared and dedicated options priced separately and different requirements around keywords. [src-2026-05-04-002]
- As a result, the source helps distinguish several Nigerian messaging patterns that are easy to conflate: ordinary outbound OTP SMS, voice OTP, premium shortcode billing, and long-code inbound workflows. [src-2026-05-04-002]
