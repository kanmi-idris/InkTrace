# Global Hack Map live cybersecurity dashboard

## Summary
Global Hack Map presents live vulnerabilities, breaches, malware, outages, and related incidents on a globe and in searchable lists. It also links to daily briefs, stack checks, a read-only MCP server, and prompt-prefilled remediation runbooks. [src-2026-11-04-032]

## Key Claims
- The site labels incident status as Confirmed, Reported, Claimed, Precautionary, or Disputed. Attacker leak-site claims are explicitly labeled unverified. [src-2026-11-04-032]
- The site says one-click links pre-fill prompts; they do not directly apply fixes. [src-2026-11-04-032]
- The live UI showed 224 sourced incidents during inspection. Counts and update times are volatile. [src-2026-11-04-032]

## Caveats
The Harvard marker showed “Confirmed · 850.5K”, while the breach list said “ShinyHunters claims Harvard breach exposing 850,500 accounts”. This is an apparent status mismatch. The breach was not independently verified in this capture. Check the source evidence before treating it as confirmed. [src-2026-11-04-032]

The site warns that absent results do not prove safety. [src-2026-11-04-032]
