# Borrow Don't Sell — Tax-Free Wealth Transfer Strategy

Tax strategy for avoiding capital gains:

1. Invest $100K → grows to $5M
2. Selling triggers tax on $4.9M gain
3. Instead: put assets in a trust
4. Borrow against the assets (loan proceeds are not taxable income)
5. Live tax-free using borrowed funds
6. Hold until death
7. Heirs inherit at stepped-up basis of $5M (cost basis resets to fair market value at death)
8. IRS gets $0 in capital gains tax — the gain is permanently extinguished via step-up in basis

Key mechanism: "borrow, don't sell" — use portfolio as collateral for loans instead of realizing gains. Step-up in basis at death eliminates the unrealized gain entirely for heirs.
