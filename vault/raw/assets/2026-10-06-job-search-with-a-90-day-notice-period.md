# Job search with a 90-day notice period

## Post and linked guide
Manali Mango says that people searching aggressively for work should read the linked guide. The post also has a photo. I did not inspect the photo.

The linked guide, published on 2026-09-13 by Engineer Turned Recruiter, addresses technology professionals in India with a 90-day notice period. It advises readers to test interview readiness before resigning, use the first 30 to 40 notice-period days for preparation, and increase applications when 45 to 60 days remain. It recommends relevant applications through multiple channels, including referrals, recruiters, networks, and direct outreach. It also advises candidates to update their stated joining timeline and learn from interviews. The guide suggests 10 to 15 relevant applications a day.

## Evidence limits
This is the guide author's job-search advice. I did not find evidence in the guide that validates its recommended timing or application volume. Its context is India and a 90-day notice period. Do not treat it as a universal job-search rule or as advice specific to Nigeria.
