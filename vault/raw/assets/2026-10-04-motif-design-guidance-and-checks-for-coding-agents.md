# Motif: Design guidance and checks for coding agents

## Product summary
Motif is a remote MCP service by ASV Labs. It connects to coding agents, including Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI, and VS Code. The site describes 738 guides across seven disciplines: websites, apps, PWAs, web apps, systems and data, motion, and lessons. Each guide is described as a principle, pattern, workflow, anti-pattern, or check, with implementation details, verification steps, and sources.

Motif says an agent can read a project's brief and approved decisions, search guidance by product type and platform, build with the user's tools and model, then run design-rule checks. The sample demonstrates checks for text contrast, touch target size, readable line width, and lab LCP. Motif supplies guidance and checks; the connected coding agent writes the app.

## Access and price
The Free plan is listed at $0 with no card or trial clock. It includes a subset of guidance, both free template families, 100 content operations per month, and one project profile. Creator is listed at $9 USD per month. It includes up to 5,000 content operations, 500 premium guidance items, 20 new premium template families, and 25 project profiles per month. There are no overage charges. Terms say unused monthly allowance does not roll over.

The homepage and pricing copy also say unused plan credit carries over. This conflicts with the terms. Confirm the rollover rule before subscribing. The free and Creator plans cover Motif usage; the connected agent's model charges remain separate.

## License and quality limits
Terms allow use and modification of delivered guidance and templates in commercial end products, including client work, and allow sharing them with one's own coding agent. They prohibit reselling or redistributing the material by itself, building a competing design library, or using it to train models. The license for material already delivered continues after cancellation.

Terms say editorial review of the library is in progress. Guides identify whether their current version was reviewed; unreviewed items may contain errors. Motif does not guarantee that output will meet accessibility, legal, or other standards. Treat checks and guidance as review aids, not certification.

The homepage sample lists education as one of ten app categories, but the inspected sample did not show detailed course screens, student dashboards, gamification, or student-progress guides. Full guidance access requires Creator, subject to its allowances.

## Privacy
The privacy policy says project briefs and design decisions are stored privately for each account. It logs usage counts, premium guide IDs, and MCP call names, outcomes, and times. It says search text and facts sent for checks are not stored, except for hashes used to recognize retries; retry records keep a response for 24 hours. Account and project data are stored in the United States. The policy says account data is deleted from live systems on deletion and leaves backups within 30 days.
