# Bachs

## Site description
Bachs presents itself as a payments and billing platform for SaaS, AI, and other digital businesses. It says merchants can accept payments, run subscriptions and usage billing, calculate tax, issue invoices, and settle in their own currency through one API and dashboard.

The site lists cards, bank transfer, African mobile money, and USDT/USDC. It says it supports 150+ currencies on the homepage. Its FAQ separately says cards support 135+ currencies; the scope of these two figures is unclear.

## Displayed pricing
The pricing page lists these rates:
- Nigerian bank transfer: 1.5%, capped at ₦2,000.
- Local cards: 2%.
- International cards: 5% + $0.40.
- Mobile money: 2% in Ghana and Kenya; 3% in other listed African markets.
- USDT/USDC: 1.5% on supported EVM-compatible networks.

The site also claims there are no setup or monthly fees, that NGN payments settle instantly, and that international card funds can be held in USD, withdrawn as USDT, or converted to NGN. These are website claims. I did not test them.

## Developer documentation
The linked docs describe a sandbox, API keys, checkout sessions, subscriptions, withdrawals, webhooks, and SDK examples. The docs say merchants can build in the sandbox before account verification. They claim most live-account reviews finish within 48 hours. I did not create an account, run code, or make a payment.

## Company and data-processing statements
The Merchant Terms identify the provider as Bachs Technologies Limited, incorporated in Nigeria, and state RC No. 9494536. The privacy policy says Bachs uses Stripe and banking partners for payment processing and settlement. It says full card numbers and CVVs are handled by processor partners, not stored on Bachs servers. These are self-published statements; I did not verify the company registration, processor relationship, bank partners, regulatory status, or security controls.

I did not find a financial-services license or named settlement-bank list in the pages inspected. This does not establish that none exists.

## Evidence limits
I inspected the homepage, pricing page, developer-docs introduction, privacy policy, and Merchant Terms. Product capabilities, pricing, settlement timing, partner relationships, customer examples, and metrics remain unverified. I did not use the service or submit information.
