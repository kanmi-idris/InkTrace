# Six Free IELTS Preparation Resources

## Evidence status
This is a user-provided IELTS resource list. Cambridge English and IDP preparation pages were checked. Claims about third-party websites, page counts, band scores, and suitability are recommendations from the thread and were not independently audited.

## Six resources
1. British Council IELTS Ready
   - Link: https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/take-ielts/prepare
   - The thread describes mock tests, skill exercises, webinars, and progress tracking.
   - The British Council page could not be fetched during this capture.

2. IELTS Liz
   - Link: https://www.ieltsliz.com/
   - The thread recommends it for Writing and Speaking lessons, model answers, band 9 samples, and vocabulary.
   - The thread claims more than 300 pages and specialist-teacher authorship. These claims were not checked.

3. Cambridge English official preparation
   - Link: https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/ielts/preparation/
   - Cambridge confirms free paper-based sample tests and links to free IELTS sample questions. It also lists additional expert preparation materials, including coursebooks and practice tests.
   - Cambridge states that it develops and writes the IELTS test.

4. IDP IELTS Prepare
   - Link: https://ielts.idp.com/prepare
   - IDP's page lists practice tests, masterclasses, podcasts, videos, articles, and familiarisation tests for Reading, Writing, Listening, and Speaking.

5. IELTS Buddy
   - Link: https://www.ieltsbuddy.com/
   - The thread recommends downloadable lessons, model essays, graph analysis, and a band-score calculator.
   - These claims were not independently checked.

6. IELTS Online Tests
   - Link: https://ieltsonlinetests.com/
   - The thread recommends full mock exams with instant estimated band scores.
   - The site behaviour and score accuracy were not independently checked.

## Additional recommendation
The thread recommends E2 IELTS on YouTube for speaking mock interviews. A reply also recommends IELTS Jacky and links to its YouTube channel.

## Suggested routine
- Use Cambridge and British Council for timed practice tests.
- Use IELTS Liz for writing models.
- Use E2 IELTS for speaking mock interviews.
- Study for 30 minutes daily instead of cramming on weekends.
