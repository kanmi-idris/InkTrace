## Free and Pro limits

The page says the free skill includes 9 chart types: Timeline, Clock, Exploded view, Annotated scene, Flowchart, Comic strip, Flow machine, Game board, and Catalogue. Tinycharts Pro includes all 28 chart types and can choose from all 28. The page lists Pro at $34.90.

The page says both tiers show logic. It says Pro includes finished templates, Chinese charts, 28 editable templates, and commercial use. The free skill is for personal, non-commercial use. The page says digital sales are final and offers no refunds.

## Skill distribution and email gate

The page lists a terminal install command that fetches a tarball and extracts it into `~/.claude/skills`. The page also offers a `.skill` file and a zip. Clicking the `.skill` link opened a form that said: “Free. Leave your email and it unlocks right away.” The form says the email will be used for updates about new skills and provides an unsubscribe contact.

No email was submitted. No skill was downloaded or installed. The terminal command was not run. Requests to guessed root URLs (`https://april-zhu.com/tinycharts.skill` and `https://april-zhu.com/tinycharts.zip`) returned 404. The site links open the email form, so no alternate paths were probed.

## Attribution and implementation caveat

The page credits art inspiration to Kevin T. Ngo's `statechart-art`, and logic to a statechart skill by Lauren Tan. The post identifies the `@poteto` handle. These credits do not prove that Tinycharts itself runs the same TLA+/TLC checker. The Tinycharts skill source was not available for inspection without email submission.
