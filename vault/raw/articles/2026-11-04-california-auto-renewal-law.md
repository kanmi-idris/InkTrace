# California Automatic Renewal Law: disclosures and consent

Captured: 2026-11-04
Publisher: California Legislative Information
Source URL: https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?article=9.&chapter=1.&division=7.&lawCode=BPC&part=3.&title
Relevant statutes: Business and Professions Code §§ 17601–17603

Section 17602 requires clear and conspicuous disclosure of automatic-renewal terms before the agreement is fulfilled and affirmative consent to those terms. The statute includes disclosures about continuation until cancellation, the cancellation policy, recurring charges and possible changes, and the renewal term or continuous-service nature.

Section 17603 says that when a business sends goods, wares, merchandise, or products under a renewal or continuous-service agreement without the required affirmative consent, those goods or products are deemed an unconditional gift. This text does not state that every renewal charge for a software subscription automatically becomes refundable.
