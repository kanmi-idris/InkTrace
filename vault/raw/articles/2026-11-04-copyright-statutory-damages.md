# U.S. Copyright Act statutory damages

Captured: 2026-11-04
Publisher: Office of the Law Revision Counsel, U.S. House of Representatives
Source URL: https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-124/t17/s504/
Statute: 17 U.S.C. §504(c)

Section 504(c) permits statutory damages of $750 to $30,000 for all infringements involved in an action with respect to any one work, as the court considers just. If the copyright owner proves, and the court finds, willful infringement, the court may increase the award to no more than $150,000. For innocent infringement, the court may reduce the award to no less than $200.

The $150,000 maximum is conditional on a court finding willful infringement. The statute applies per work, not automatically per upload or per image shown in a profile. Who is liable and whether a safe harbor applies are separate questions.
