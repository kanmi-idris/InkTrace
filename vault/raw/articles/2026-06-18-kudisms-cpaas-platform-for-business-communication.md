# KudiSMS - CPaaS Platform for Business Communication

KudiSMS - Powerful CPaaS Solutions for Business Communication

## Overview
KudiSMS is a Communications Platform as a Service (CPaaS) that enables businesses to communicate with customers across multiple channels: SMS, Email, WhatsApp, and Voice messaging. Based in Nigeria, serves businesses across Africa and beyond.

## Communication Channels

### SMS Messaging
- OTPs & authentication
- Transactional updates
- Promotional campaigns
- Reminders & notifications

### Email Messaging
- Newsletters
- Order confirmations
- Account updates
- Promotional offers
- Email OTP

### WhatsApp Messaging (Cloud API)
- New user onboarding
- Account verification OTPs
- Appointment reminders
- Order updates

### Voice Messaging
- Pre-recorded voice messages
- Text-to-Speech (TTS) calls
- Customer service alerts
- Appointment reminders
- Verification & OTP calls
- Promotional voice messages
