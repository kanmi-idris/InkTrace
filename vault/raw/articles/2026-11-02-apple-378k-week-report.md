# Mobilegamer.biz report: Apple App Review delays and volume

Captured: 2026-11-02
Publisher: Mobilegamer.biz
Article date: 2026-10-20
Source URL: https://mobilegamer.biz/app-store-review-times-are-being-dragged-down-by-a-flood-of-ai-made-apps-and-games/

The report says multiple small and mid-sized game developers described review waits of several weeks. It quotes several anonymous studios. One said five apps had waited over a week. Another said reviews took three to five days, even when expedited. The report also says one mid-sized studio had not seen slower update reviews for a game that had been on the App Store for about five years. These are attributed reports, not a representative survey.

Apple told Mobilegamer.biz that it had prioritized reviews of updates to existing apps, expanded automation, strengthened abuse detection, and added safeguards. Apple said that in the prior 12 weeks it processed an average of 378,000 app submissions per week, with an average review time of 1.5 days for all apps and 14 hours for minor updates. Apple said some new apps take longer when they require additional scrutiny.

The article describes the bottleneck as appearing to affect new app and game submissions. It does not publish a statistical distribution for first-time developers or substantiate every specific wait-length anecdote.
