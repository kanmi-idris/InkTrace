# 9to5Mac report: Apple App Review submissions and timing

Captured: 2026-11-02
Publisher: 9to5Mac
Article date: 2026-04-06
Source URL: https://9to5mac.com/2026/04/06/app-store-sees-84-surge-in-new-apps-as-ai-coding-tools-take-off/

The article reports Apple's statement that, over the preceding 12 weeks, App Review processed more than 200,000 app submissions per week. Apple also said 90% of submissions were processed within 48 hours and the average review time was 1.5 days. The article says Apple increasingly used AI tools to assist human reviewers, while a human still reviewed every submission.

This is a press report of a spokesperson's statement, not an Apple-published dated metric page. “App submissions” is the unit used in the report.
