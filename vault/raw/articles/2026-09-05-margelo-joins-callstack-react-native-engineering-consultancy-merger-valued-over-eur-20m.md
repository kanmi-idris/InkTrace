# Margelo Joins Callstack: React Native Engineering Consultancy Merger (Valued Over EUR 20M)

## Source overview
This captures a business announcement published 2026-09-01 on Margelo's own blog, authored by Marc Rousavy (creator of VisionCamera and Nitro Modules), stating that Margelo is joining Callstack in a transaction the post says values Margelo at more than EUR 20 million. Margelo and Callstack are both React Native consultancy/engineering firms already referenced elsewhere in this vault through their individual open-source projects and technical blog posts (Nitro Modules, VisionCamera, NitroFetch, and various Margelo engineering write-ups already captured separately).

## What the announcement states
The post frames the combination as bringing together Margelo's performance-focused engineering and open-source work (specifically naming Nitro Modules, VisionCamera, and react-native-mmkv as Margelo's best-known projects) with Callstack's stated decade of experience helping large organizations adopt, scale, and operate React Native at an enterprise level. The stated rationale is that as AI makes basic implementation work faster, the harder problems (architecture, performance, native integration, validation, and release safety) become relatively more important, and the combined team intends to focus on those production-readiness concerns rather than treating the merger as primarily about faster feature delivery.

The author states that Margelo's existing open-source libraries will remain open source, and that he will continue to lead their development while also helping shape the combined company's broader offering. The post links out to a fuller announcement hosted on Callstack's own site, which was not independently fetched in this capture.

## Evidence boundary
This capture reflects only Margelo's own blog post announcing the merger. The linked Callstack-hosted announcement, and the actual deal terms beyond the stated valuation figure, were not independently verified in this capture.
