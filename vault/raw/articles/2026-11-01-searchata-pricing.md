# Searchata pricing snapshot

Captured: 2026-11-01
Source URL: https://searchata.com/pricing
Publisher: Searchata

## Published plans

- Monthly: $12 USD per month.
- Yearly: $79 USD per year.
- New subscribers can start a seven-day trial. A card is required, and the user chooses a plan during setup.
- The selected plan renews automatically after the trial unless cancelled.

The plans include Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, all properties accessible to connected accounts, performance/indexing/sitemap tools, assistant connections for Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, and Claude Code, and no monthly MCP-call cap for normal interactive use. Searchata states that per-minute, concurrency, source-provider, and performance-tool limits still apply.

The page says an AI assistant account is separate and is not included in Searchata pricing.

## Evidence limits

Prices and plan terms can change. Check the live pricing page before purchase. I did not start a trial or enter billing details.
