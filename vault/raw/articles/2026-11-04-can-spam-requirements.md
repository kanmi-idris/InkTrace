# FTC CAN-SPAM compliance guide

Captured: 2026-11-04
Publisher: Federal Trade Commission
Source URL: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business

The FTC says CAN-SPAM applies to commercial messages, including promotional email, not only bulk email. Its guide says commercial email must include a valid physical postal address and a clear opt-out method. The sender must honor opt-out requests within 10 business days. A launch announcement to a waitlist may be commercial depending on its primary purpose and content.

The FTC guide states that each separate email in violation may be subject to a civil penalty of up to $53,088. This is a maximum, not an automatic per-email fine. The sender, promoted company, and others may face responsibility depending on the facts.
