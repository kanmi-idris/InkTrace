# OSI² GitLab - Open Source Imaging Initiative

https://gitlab.com/osii/ — Open Source Imaging Initiative (OSI²) GitLab group. Development space for the Open Source Imaging Initiative (OSI²), an open-source ecosystem for MRI technology.

Hosted projects include:
- osii/magnet/30cm-halbach-magnet — 50mT permanent magnet design (ROMA array, ~€10k, CERN OHL W v2)
- And other open-source MRI hardware/software projects

Related: opensourceimaging.org (website), OSI² e.V. (association). The OSI² ONE Magnet and OSII Mini at Utrecht University are derived from this group's work.
