# My Little Storybook

## Source overview
My Little Storybook is a Lusion Monthly Experiment about a bird family crossing a river.

## Design direction
The experiment uses stylized nature with a concept inspired by Japanese anime. It is presented as a visual storytelling experience.

## Production context
Lusion describes its monthly experiments as internal challenges. Two or more creatives collaborate, and each team member can spend no more than two hours per day on the experiment.
