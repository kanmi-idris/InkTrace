# The Register: reports of long App Store review waits

Captured: 2026-11-02
Publisher: The Register
Article date: 2026-10-22; updated 2026-10-27
Source URL: https://www.theregister.com/applications/2026/10/22/apple-app-store-review-process-hits-a-traffic-jam/5304802

The Register reports that Apple Developer Forums included threads describing waits of about three weeks and waits beginning October 8. It cites a D1 Tech forum post saying its app had been waiting since September 19. At the article date, October 22, that was 33 days. The Register also links to a Reddit post titled “My app has been waiting for App Store review for almost a month. Is this normal?”

These are individual developer reports and forum posts, not a representative sample. The Register states that the typical review is a day or two and quotes Apple's public 50% under 24 hours and 90% under 48 hours wording. Apple later provided a statement to the publication about standards and evolving the process, but the statement did not provide a separate first-time-developer queue statistic.
