# LiteReality App - App Store

## Source overview
LiteReality is an iOS LiDAR room scanner. The App Store listing describes it as a free Graphics & Design app by zhening huang.

## Captured outputs
- Room geometry from Apple RoomPlan, exported as USDZ.
- RGB-D keyframes with camera intrinsics, extrinsics, and tracking state.
- Camera poses and per-frame timing.
- Point cloud and scene metadata.

## Compatibility
The listing states that a LiDAR-equipped iPhone or iPad is required. It lists iOS 16 or later and Apple Silicon Mac compatibility through the App Store listing.
