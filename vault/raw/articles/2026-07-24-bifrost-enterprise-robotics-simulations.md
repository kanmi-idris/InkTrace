# Bifrost - Enterprise Robotics Simulations

LinkedIn post by Aravind Kandiah (Jul 2026, 817 reactions): Bifrost solves the real2sim problem for enterprise robotics teams. Makes simulations look realistic, run in minutes, accurately model deployment scenarios.

Key breakthroughs:
- High-fidelity simulations run in 30 minutes instead of 12 hours
- GPU orchestration + graphics optimizations

Two technical breakthroughs:
1. Articulated 3D generation: TRELLIS matured static 3D asset generation. Articraft goes further — produces articulated, simulation-ready assets from a prompt or reference image. Week of artist time → minutes.
2. Gaussian splat environments: World Labs' Marble converts images/video of real spaces into explorable splat environments for direct simulation use. SceniX leverages these for highly correlated real-sim policy evaluation metrics. GaRe relights splats for lighting diversity. Recreating a site is now same-day.

Bifrost: end-to-end real2sim pipelines. Creates digital cousins of real-world scenarios. Drop in a video → get customized simulation environment.
