# AppLlama: Top-Grossing iOS App Onboarding and Paywall Reference Library

Source checked: 2026-09-27.

## Scope
- AppLlama is a screen-by-screen reference library for onboarding, paywall, and product flows from top-grossing iOS apps.
- The homepage currently reports more than 58,500 screens from more than 1,330 apps, with new apps added weekly. Its visible directory lists 1,338 apps. These counts are site-reported and can change.

## Reference data
- App entries expose store category and rank context, app name, short positioning text, and site-reported revenue, download, rating, and review signals.
- Entries also show counts for screens, still images, videos, and captured flows.
- The library is useful for comparing acquisition, onboarding, and monetization journeys at screen level.

## Evidence and limits
- Revenue, download, rating, review, directory-size, and trusted-customer statements are claims displayed by the site.
- The homepage focuses on iOS examples, so its patterns should not be treated as complete Android coverage.
- Use the library as a visual research source, not as an independently audited market dataset.
