# Apple App Store page: App Review weekly volume

Captured: 2026-11-02
Publisher: Apple
Source URL: https://www.apple.com/app-store/
Page publication date: not shown in captured page

Apple's App Store page states: “Every week, nearly 500 dedicated experts around the world review over 130K apps.” The page also states that in 2024 Apple rejected more than 1.9 million app submissions for reasons including privacy violations and fraudulent activity.

The page does not state when the 130K weekly figure was measured. It uses “apps,” not the “app submissions” wording used in later media reports.
