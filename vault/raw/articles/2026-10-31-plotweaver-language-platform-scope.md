# PlotWeaver broader language-platform coverage

Captured: 2026-10-31
Source URL: https://www.plotweaver.app/languages
Publisher: PlotWeaver

## Platform-level figures

The languages page describes STORM-OS V3 as covering 73 African languages across 44 countries, with five languages having native voices, speech-to-text, and translation. It lists 1.1 billion speakers across the stated language coverage.

The page names Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, Nigerian Pidgin, and Nigerian English among the five languages with native voices, speech-to-text, and translation. It separately lists languages across the broader platform catalogue.

## Scope distinction

These are platform-level STORM-OS V3 figures. The separate STORM Ensemble 3.0 product page states 64 languages and 166 voices for that speech synthesis model. Do not merge the 73-language platform count with the 64-language model count. See [src-2026-10-31-002].

## Evidence limits

These are publisher statements. The page does not provide an independent language-by-language evaluation. The model page is [src-2026-10-31-002].
