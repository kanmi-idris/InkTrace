# nRF Connect for Mobile: Bluetooth LE Development and Diagnostics

Source checked: 2026-09-27.

## Scope
- nRF Connect for Mobile is Nordic Semiconductor's generic Bluetooth Low Energy development and diagnostics application.
- The listing describes BLE discovery, advertisement parsing, RSSI graphs, CSV and Excel export, connection, service and characteristic inspection, reads, writes, notifications, and indications.
- It also supports BLE advertising, GATT server configuration, Nordic UART Service, Device Firmware Update, McuMgr, macros, and XML-defined automated tests.
- The listing reports 1M+ downloads and an update dated December 12, 2024.

## Safety and evidence limits
- This is primarily a device development, debugging, and test utility.
- Read and write operations can change connected hardware state. Use them only on devices owned or authorized by the user.
- Firmware and automated-test support depend on the target device and profile.
- No application was installed or connected to a device.
