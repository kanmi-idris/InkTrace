# OSI² ONE MR Scanner - Open Source Low-Field MRI

https://www.opensourceimaging.org/project/osii-one/ — OSI² ONE MR Scanner: full open-source low-field MRI scanner system (complete system, vs the magnet-only project). Modular design, ongoing development.

Specs:
- Permanent magnet B0=50mT
- Head and extremities (inner bore ~30cm)
- Spatial resolution 1.5×1.5×5mm³
- Portable, ~150kg
- Standard power socket (230V/16A)
- Estimated material cost: €25k–60k depending on config

Licenses: Hardware CERN-OHL-W, Software mostly GPL-3.0
Repository: gitlab.com/osii/
Communication: Matrix (#osii:matrix.org)

News: Hannover Messe 2024, Build Workshop recording published 2023, First presented Jan 2023.

Publications: On-site construction in Africa (NMR in Biomedicine), 3D MRI in Halbach array (JMR 2019), In vivo 3D brain at 50mT (MRM 2021), Open source medical devices (Springer 2019), Open-source MRI for global collaboration (NMR in Biomedicine).

Funding: European Partnership on Metrology (A4IM project, Horizon Europe), dtec.bw (Bundeswehr / NextGeneration EU).

Related projects: MFS – Magnetic Field Simulator, KomaMRI.
