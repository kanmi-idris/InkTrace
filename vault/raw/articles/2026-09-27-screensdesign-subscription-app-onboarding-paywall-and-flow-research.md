# ScreensDesign: Subscription-App Onboarding, Paywall, and Flow Research

Source checked: 2026-09-27.

## Scope
- ScreensDesign presents a research library for top-grossing subscription apps.
- The homepage reports more than 2,715 apps and positions the library around onboarding, paywalls, store screens, full flows, and post-purchase experiences.

## Research features
- Users can inspect full sessions that run from an app's first screen through a paywall and the steps after purchase.
- Search and browsing are organized around app collections and flow examples.
- The site also promotes a creation product for generating onboarding flows, paywalls, and full app screens, with a handoff to AI coding agents.
- Revenue and app-performance signals appear beside some app examples, which supports pattern research tied to commercial outcomes.

## Evidence and limits
- App counts, revenue figures, customer lists, and conversion-oriented language are vendor-reported marketing claims.
- The homepage focuses on iOS and subscription-app examples, so the library may not represent every platform or product category.
- Treat observed screens and flows as design evidence, but verify business metrics independently.
