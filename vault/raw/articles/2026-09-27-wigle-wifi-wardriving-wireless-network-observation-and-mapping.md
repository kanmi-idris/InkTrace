# WiGLE WiFi Wardriving: Wireless Network Observation and Mapping

Source checked: 2026-09-27.

## Scope
- WiGLE WiFi Wardriving is an open-source Android client for observing, positioning, and displaying wireless networks.
- The listing describes GPS-based location estimates, a local observation database, maps with WiGLE data overlays, and optional uploads to WiGLE.net.
- It supports CSV and KML export, Bluetooth GPS, and audio or text-to-speech alerts.
- The listing reports 1M+ downloads and an update dated August 14, 2026.

## Safety and evidence limits
- The listing explicitly says that WiGLE data is not a list of networks to use.
- Wireless observations can expose location and network information. Use local collection and uploads only with appropriate consent and legal authority.
- Open-source status, download counts, and database-size claims are source or store statements and can change.
- No application was installed or used for collection.
