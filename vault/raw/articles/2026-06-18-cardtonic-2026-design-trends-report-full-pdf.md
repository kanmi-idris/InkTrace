A 2026 Report on Design Trends

Upon a Time

Historically, identifying good design was simple. If the layout was clean
and the execution was polished, it was ready. The primary metric for
success was technical perfection, but that reality is shifting rapidly as Al
reshapes the industry. Today, high-quality logos, posters, and videos can
be generated in seconds, making visual perfection so common that it no
longer commands the attention it once did. As traditional mastery of tools
like Photoshop becomes less of a differentiator, many creatives are left
wondering if their roles are becoming obsolete.

At Cardtonic, we recognize the weight of this shift. Rather than watching
the creative community panic from the sidelines, our design team chose
to investigate the evolving landscape. This report is the result of that deep
dive. It is an effort to cut through the Al hype, analyze its true impact on
design, and provide clarity on what the future holds for the industry.
