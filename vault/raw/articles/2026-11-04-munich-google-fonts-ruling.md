# Munich Regional Court ruling on Google Fonts and IP disclosure

Captured: 2026-11-04
Publisher: Bavarian state legal information service, reproducing Landgericht München I judgment
Source URL: https://www.gesetze-bayern.de/Content/Document/Y-300-Z-BECKRS-B-2022-N-612
Decision: Landgericht München I, 20 January 2022, 3 O 17493/20

The judgment held that the website operator unlawfully disclosed one plaintiff's dynamic IP address to Google when the page loaded Google Fonts from Google's server without consent. It ordered the operator to stop that disclosure, provide information, and pay the plaintiff €100 plus interest in damages.

The decision is a single German first-instance civil judgment. The €100 was damages to the plaintiff, not a general statutory fine or a standard per-visitor penalty. The court noted that fonts could be hosted locally without sending the visitor's IP address to Google.
