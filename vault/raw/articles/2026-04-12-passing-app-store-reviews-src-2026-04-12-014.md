Launching an app on the App Store is a significant milestone for any startup. However, the journey from development to deployment can be challenging and filled with potential hurdles. Having guided numerous startups through this process, we've identified key areas where attention to detail can mean the difference between swift approval and frustrating delays. At Povio , we specialize in mobile app development, helping businesses build high-quality apps that pass App Store reviews seamlessly.
Dive in part 1 of the essential tips, enriched with insights from our experiences, to help ensure your app navigates the review process smoothly.
Establish a Dedicated Website for Legal Policies
Before submission, it's important to have a dedicated website hosting your Privacy Policy and Terms of Use . Apple requires direct URLs to these documents during the app submission process. These policies must be clear, accessible, and specifically tailored to your app's functionalities, especially if you collect user data.
Why It's Crucial: Lack of accessible legal documents is a common cause for immediate rejection.
Actionable Step: Create a simple website or a dedicated page on your existing site to host these documents. Ensure the URLs are live and accessible without requiring any login.
Additional Insight: Regularly update these policies to reflect any changes in data handling or app features. Transparency builds user trust and aligns with Apple's emphasis on user privacy. Implement 'Sign in with Apple' for Social Logins
If your app includes third-party sign-in options like Facebook or Google , Apple mandates the inclusion of 'Sign in with Apple' . This feature provides users with enhanced privacy controls and a seamless login experience.
Common Mistake: Omitting 'Sign in with Apple' when offering other social logins always leads to rejection.
Solution: Integrate 'Sign in with Apple' using Apple's authentication services. Ensure it offers the same capabilities as other login options.
First-Hand Example: A client app was initially rejected for missing this feature. After integration, not only was the app approved, but user sign-ups increased due to the convenience and trust associated with Apple's login system. Provide an Easily Accessible Account Deletion Option
Apps that allow account creation must also allow users to delete their accounts from within the app.
User Trust Factor: Providing an easy way to delete an account enhances transparency and user control over personal data.
Implementation Tip: Place the account deletion option within the app's settings or profile section. Ensure the process is straightforward and doesn't require external actions like sending emails or making phone calls.
Important Note: The deletion process should be complete and permanent, removing all user data from your servers, in compliance with data protection regulations like GDPR. Prioritize Clear and Intuitive User Experience (UX)
An app with a confusing UX can frustrate users and raise red flags during the review process.
Reviewer's Perspective: Apple reviewers assess apps for usability and adherence to design standards.
Action Steps:
Simplify Onboarding: Guide users through initial setup with clear instructions or interactive tutorials.
Consistent Navigation: Maintain a logical flow throughout the app to prevent user confusion.
Accessibility: Ensure your app is usable by people with disabilities by supporting features like VoiceOver .
