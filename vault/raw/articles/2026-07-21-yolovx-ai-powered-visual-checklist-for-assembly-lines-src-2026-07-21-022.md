Title: YOLOvX — AI-Powered Visual Checklist for Assembly Lines
Author: YOLOvX (via LinkedIn post by Patrick Deschere)
Retrieved: 2026-07-21

---

A proof-of-concept system that uses real-time object detection to track parts during assembly — monitoring their location and how they fit together — then automatically updates a digital checklist. Operators receive clear visual confirmation of completed steps before advancing, reducing missed actions in repetitive tasks.

- Leverages a fine-tuned **RF-DETR** model trained on targeted labeled frames for reliable detection in complex scenes.

## Use Cases
- Manufacturing and assembly lines
- Quality control and inspection processes
- Maintenance and repair workflows
- Training and onboarding for technical procedures
- Any multi-step manual process where consistency and verification matter
