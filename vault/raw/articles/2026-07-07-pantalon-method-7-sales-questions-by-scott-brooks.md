# Pantalon Method — 7 Sales Questions by Scott Brooks

X thread by Scott Brooks (@scotti_brooks) — 7-question sales method attributed to Pantalon for moving prospects from mild interest to commitment without pitching.

1. "Why might you want to fix this?" — Ask for their reasons to buy instead of listing yours. People believe their own reasons, not yours.

2. "On a scale of 1 to 10, how ready are you?" — Unusual question forces real thought. Gets an honest self-rated number on their interest.

3. "Why didn't you pick a lower number?" — The key move. Asking why not higher makes them defend skepticism. Asking why not lower makes them verbalize their actual interest.

4. "Imagine it's handled. What's different for you?" — Let the buyer picture and describe the after in their own words. Becomes their idea, not your pitch.

5. "Why does that matter to you?" — Layer deeper after every reason. 2-3 rounds and mild interest becomes personal motivation.

6. [Thread implies step 6 context but the text jumps from 5 to 7 in the thread]

7. "What's one small step you could take?" — End on the smallest safe move, not the biggest ask. First small yes is hardest to walk back.

Core insight: "Not one of them is a pitch. They're just questions that make another person hear their own reasons out loud." Cited as backed by Yale research — people move faster on reasons they articulate themselves.
