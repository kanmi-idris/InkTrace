# BoltAI and PDF Pals product pages

Captured: 2026-11-01
Sources: https://boltai.com/ and https://pdfpals.com/
Publishers: BoltAI and PDF Pals

## BoltAI

BoltAI describes itself as a native Mac AI workspace, built with SwiftUI and AppKit. The page lists macOS 13 or later and Intel and Apple Silicon support.

## PDF Pals

PDF Pals describes itself as a native AI PDF assistant for Mac. The page lists macOS 13 or later, with version 2 requiring macOS 14 or later.

## Evidence limits

These product pages establish the apps' published Mac positioning, not the quality or completeness of their Windows ports. The port is reported by Daniel Nguyen in [src-2026-11-01-006].
