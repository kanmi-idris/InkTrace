# Framer AEO Scanner

Captured from user-supplied product copy and the official public page on 2026-05-01.

Canonical URL:
https://www.framer.com/aeo

---

User-supplied positioning:

- Framer dropped an AEO Scanner.
- Paste a URL to see what AI engines are not reading and how to fix it.
- The framing is that a site can appear visible to humans and search engines while still being effectively invisible to AI answer engines.

Official page highlights captured from the public page:

- The page headline asks: “Can AI find your website?”
- Framer positions the tool around “Answer Engine Optimization (AEO).”
- The page says AI systems like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini now answer questions directly and that many sites are not prepared for that shift.
- The scanner is described as assessing one URL and using AI, with an explicit note that results may not be perfect and should be treated as a guide rather than a final answer.
- The page says its scores reflect Framer’s own methodology and may differ from other AEO reports.
- The AEO explanation on the page defines the practice as making content easier for AI systems to find, understand, and quote.
- Framer argues that structure, metadata, and trust signals matter because AI models favor clear metadata, structured content, and authoritative sources.
- The page also claims that many searches now end without a click, increasing the importance of being surfaced directly in AI-generated answers.
