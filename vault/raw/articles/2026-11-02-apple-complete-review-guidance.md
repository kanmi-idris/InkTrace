# Apple Help: Provide information for a complete review

Captured: 2026-11-02
Publisher: Apple Developer
Source URL: https://developer.apple.com/help/app-review/before-submitting-for-review/complete-review

Apple says a complete submission can move through review without a rejection for missing information. It says an incomplete submission is rejected for missing information and must be resubmitted for a new review.

Apple asks for general information about the app's concept, features, audience, and tested devices and operating systems. It asks developers to provide current demo account credentials for account-based features and add credentials for other account types in the Notes field.

For apps that require specific hardware, Apple says to provide a video, not a screen recording, showing the app running on a physical Apple device as it pairs and interacts with the hardware. This video requirement is conditional, not a general instruction for every first app.
