Apple can't keep up with app review right now, and you can see it in their own numbers

Their website says about 500 people review 130K apps a week

In April they said it was 200K a week. Last month, 378,000

That's almost 3x the volume still listed on their website. Developers report new apps sitting in Waiting for Review for weeks, some over a month, while Apple says minor updates average 14 hours

Apple's response is that the average is still 1.5 days. Sure, but averages hide the queue. If you're a first-time dev with a brand new app, you are the queue

What I'd do if I were submitting my first app this month: add a demo login, write real notes, and link a screen recording the first time. Apple says it themselves, incomplete submissions get rejected. And a rejection right now can cost you weeks
