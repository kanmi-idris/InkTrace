Product positioning captured from the public page:

- Product name shown on the page: `Space`
- Tagline: “Infinite space on your computer.”
- Current availability note: “Now in private beta on macOS”

Core product claims captured from the public page:

- The product is presented as a way to increase disk space by streaming and editing very large files in native apps without fully downloading them.
- It claims the user can search across every connected drive at once from a single shortcut.
- It exposes files in Finder at `/Volumes/Space`.
- It is positioned as working with existing creative applications including Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro, After Effects, and Photoshop.

Streaming and caching notes captured from the public page:

- The page says Space streams only the byte ranges an editor reads.
- A 50 GB file is presented as potentially using about 800 MB of local cache for an entire session.
- The emphasis is on opening and scrubbing large media files as if they were local while avoiding full local copies.
