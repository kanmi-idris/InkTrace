# FTC COPPA FAQ: coverage and civil penalties

Captured: 2026-11-04
Publisher: Federal Trade Commission
Source URL: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions

FTC FAQ guidance says COPPA applies to operators of child-directed websites or services and to general-audience operators with actual knowledge that they collect, use, or disclose personal information from children under 13. It says the Rule does not require general-audience services to ask visitors' ages. A service that chooses neutral age screening may rely on the age supplied, subject to the FAQ's details.

The FTC FAQ states that a court may impose civil penalties of up to $53,088 per violation. It says the amount sought or assessed depends on factors such as seriousness, prior violations, number of children, type and use of information, sharing, and operator size. This is a maximum, not an automatic charge each time a child registers.
