Service and pairing model captured from the README:

- `simdeck pair` starts or refreshes a LaunchAgent-backed service.
- It prints local, LAN, and Tailscale URLs when available.
- It displays a QR code with a `simdeck://pair` link.
- Service restarts preserve the token unless the service is reset.
- CLI commands can reuse the same warm service.

Inspector details captured from the README:

- NativeScript apps can expose their logical hierarchy and raw UIKit backing views by calling:

```ts
startSimDeckInspector({ port: 4310 });
```

- The runtime connects to the SimDeck server over WebSocket.
- The Rust server prefers connected NativeScript inspectors for hierarchy requests and falls back to Swift TCP inspector or native accessibility when needed.
