# Apple Developer App Review page

Captured: 2026-11-02
Publisher: Apple Developer
Source URL: https://developer.apple.com/distribute/app-review

Apple says its review process typically reviews at least 50% of submissions in less than 24 hours and 90% in less than 48 hours. Apple says it reviews apps, app updates, app bundles, in-app purchases, and in-app events submitted to App Store Connect.

Apple says incomplete App Review information may delay review or prevent a submission from passing. For sign-in features, it asks developers to provide a valid demo account. It asks for details about special configurations. For features that need an environment that is hard to replicate or specific hardware, it says to be prepared to provide a demo video or the hardware.

Apple notes that over 40% of unresolved issues relate to Guideline 2.1, App Completeness. These are Apple's published figures and guidance, not a guarantee for an individual submission.
