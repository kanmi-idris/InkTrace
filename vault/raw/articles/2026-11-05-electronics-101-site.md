## First lesson inspected

The Basics section contains 9 lessons. Lesson 1 is “Open and closed circuits.” Its overview says electricity flows around a complete loop from battery positive, through the bulb, and back to negative. It says a gap anywhere stops current everywhere.

The lesson has Overview, Components, Circuit, and Maths tabs. The Maths tab shows `I = V ÷ R = 3.0 V ÷ 10 Ω = 300 mA`. The Circuit tab shows a diagram and a switch control. The workbench exposes turn, zoom, reset, and overhead-view controls. Its instructions also describe drag or WASD to pan, Q and E to rotate, and scroll to zoom.

A browser click on the visible switch control left its displayed state as “closed.” I did not confirm that the switch changes the simulated circuit. I did not test every lesson or every language.
