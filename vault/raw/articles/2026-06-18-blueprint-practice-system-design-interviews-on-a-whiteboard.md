# Blueprint - Practice System Design Interviews on a Whiteboard

Blueprint - Practice System Design Interviews on a Whiteboard

## Overview
Blueprint (systemsdesign.site) turns system design into puzzles you can practice, with AI grading your architecture. Drag components onto a whiteboard, wire them together, and an AI interviewer grades your design. Built for interview prep, design reviews, and sharpening architecture instincts.

## Key Features
- Free to play, no login required
- Vendor-neutral component palette (generic primitives: cache, queue, gateway, object store, stream processor — not DynamoDB, SQS, etc.)
- 30 floors (hand-built interview problems) with branching paths
- AI-generated puzzles (infinite practice after completing the tower)
- Whiteboard with drag-and-drop components
- Progress tracking across devices (with login)
- Shareable whiteboard sessions
- Live collaborative sessions with cursors

## Floor Progression
- Core climb, forks into Scale or Realtime, crests summit, fans into 4 advanced regions, capstones fuse everything
- Example problems: URL Shortener, Rate Limiter, Social Feed, Realtime Chat, Video Hosting, Search Autocomplete

## Target Audiences
- Students: branching progression of 30 hand-built problems + AI-generated puzzles
- Builders: reusable whiteboard with full component kit, share links for remixing
- Interviewers: dynamically generated problems, live shareable sessions with cursor tracking

## Tech Positioning
- Free, no login required for basic use
- Log in to save progress, cleared floors, and diagrams across devices
- Feedback & bugs via GitHub issues (github.com/AlexsJones/blueprint)
