# U.S. Copyright Office DMCA designated-agent FAQ

Captured: 2026-11-04
Publisher: U.S. Copyright Office
Source URL: https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/faq.html

The Copyright Office says the current fee is $6 per designated-agent registration, amendment, or resubmission. It says a service provider seeking the DMCA §512 safe harbors must designate an agent. Failure to keep a designation current can put the service provider at risk of losing those safe-harbor protections.

The safe harbor is a limitation on liability for certain qualifying online service providers based on users' infringement. It requires meeting statutory conditions. The registration is not a blanket license for user content and does not mean that every app that accepts a profile image must register an agent.
