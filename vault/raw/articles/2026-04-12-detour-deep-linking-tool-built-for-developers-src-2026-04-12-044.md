Detour is an open-source deep linking library: lightweight, developer-first, and free to start. Soon with self-hosting, SSO, and more features. Read the docs .
Get started
Everything you need for deep links
From simple redirects to complex user acquisition funnels,
Detour provides the tools developers need.
Developer-friendly
Most deep link tools are built for marketers, not engineers. Detour takes a different path – open-source, lightweight, and technically sound, so you can rely on it in production.
Link routing made smart
Detour handles deferred deep links with precision – resolving context across installs, preserving parameters, and getting users to the right screen without extra setup.
Analytics and insights
Understand how your links perform with lightweight, developer-friendly analytics – privacy-aware, and built to integrate seamlessly with your workflow.
Custom domains
Your brand shouldn't be hidden behind a generic URL. Detour allows you to map deep links to your own infrastructure with support for Universal Links and App Links.
Multiple platforms
Integrate deep linking across the entire mobile ecosystem. We provide dedicated SDKs for iOS, Android, React Native, and Flutter.
Self-hosting
Available soon
Deploy the entire Detour stack – dashboard and infrastructure – on your own servers. Ideal for high-compliance sectors where data privacy is non-negotiable.
