NASA's Earth Science Data Systems (ESDS) Program provides open access to NASA's archive of Earth science data.

## Key Statistics
- **178+ petabytes** of Earth science data available
- **10+ million** distinct data and service users
- **7.4 billion** files distributed to end users
- Projected archive growth to **500+ petabytes by 2030**

## Data Centers (12 DAACs)
1. Alaska Satellite Facility (ASF) DAAC

## Science Topics
Atmosphere, Biosphere, Climate Indicators, Cryosphere, Human Dimensions, Land Surface, Ocean, Solid Earth, Sun-Earth Interactions, Terrestrial Hydrosphere

## Key Data Tools
- **Earthdata Search** — data discovery, search, comparison, visualization, access
- **Worldview** — interactive global satellite imagery browser
- **FIRMS** — Fire Information for Resource Management System
- **Giovanni** — online data visualization and analysis
- **AppEEARS** — extract and explore analysis-ready samples
- **VEDA** — Visualization, Exploration, and Data Analysis
- **GUARDIAN** — GNSS-based Upper Atmospheric Realtime Disaster Information and Alert Network (NRT ionospheric monitoring)
- **Earthdata GIS** — cloud-native, GIS-ready data services (200+ raster/feature services)
- **Ocean Color Level 3 and 4 Browser**
- **Vertex**
- **Earthdata Forum** — community Q&A

## Web Unification Project
Migrating all NASA Earth science data sites into Earthdata from now until end of 2026. Not all data/resources appear here until then.
