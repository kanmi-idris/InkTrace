# Mayron v. Google LLC: California automatic-renewal gift remedy

Captured: 2026-11-04
Court: California Court of Appeal, Sixth Appellate District
Decision: 11 September 2020
Case: H044592
Source URL: https://law.justia.com/cases/california/court-of-appeal/2020/h044592.html

The Court of Appeal interpreted California Business and Professions Code §17603's unconditional-gift language as a right to retain a product already received. The court said the provision did not create a right to pursue a refund of fees paid. It affirmed dismissal of the plaintiff's claims in that case.

The case predates California's 2025 amendments to the Automatic Renewal Law. It is cited here narrowly for the gift provision's distinction from an automatic refund; it does not resolve every current subscription dispute or every possible claim.
