# GeekNative's case for native apps with shared specs

## Source

- https://geekyants.github.io/geeknative/blog/cross-platform-without-cross-platform-technology/

## Article argument

The article says coding agents reduce the cost of writing three implementations, but consistency remains difficult. GeekNative's proposed approach is to share product and feature specifications, design tokens, copy, icons, and test criteria, while generating separate SwiftUI, Jetpack Compose, and React codebases.

It says the approach is not the right fit for every team. Teams with a working React Native or Flutter app, a strong JavaScript or Dart team, or a need for desktop apps may have good reasons to stay with their current stack. The article says the strongest fit is for teams that value native apps and already use coding agents.

## Evidence limits

This is GeekyAnts' own product argument. It is not an independent comparison of cost, speed, or app quality. I did not build or test a project.
