# Developer Tooling Catalog

## Summary
Outside the strongest AI and React Native clusters, the reviewed links still form a useful secondary catalog: WebView integration patterns, framework ergonomics, localization architecture, programmatic video, UX heuristics, software design patterns, App Store review advice, and a smaller set of product or marketing references. ## Contradictions
- The Bun or Elysia material is internally split between ergonomic appeal and perceived runtime risk, but the evidence is discussion-heavy and anecdotal rather than coming from formal incident reports or long-horizon production studies. [src-2026-04-23-002]
- The money-modeling source is also internally split: the main advice says to use integer minor units everywhere, while the follow-up argues that some business domains require more than two decimal places internally, so “always use kobo integers” is not a universal rule. [src-2026-05-03-001]
- The reverse-OTP source also has an evidence gap: its architecture sketch is concrete, but its claims about Nigerian shortcode feasibility, provider options, and regulatory practicality are not backed by direct primary documentation in the supplied conversation. [src-2026-05-03-006]
- The Medium reverse-OTP article has a different weakness: it explains the concept clearly, but many of its strongest claims are entangled with marketing for ReverseOTP.com rather than neutral comparative evidence. [src-2026-05-04-001]
- The Greenspek pricing source is narrower but stronger on numbers: it provides concrete commercial pricing, yet it is still one vendor’s rate card rather than a complete market survey of Nigerian SMS and shortcode infrastructure. [src-2026-05-04-002]
