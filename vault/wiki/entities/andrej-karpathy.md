# Andrej Karpathy

## Who or What
Andrej Karpathy is the author of the `LLM Wiki` gist that outlines the persistent wiki pattern used as the conceptual starting point for InkTrace. [src-2026-04-12-001]

## Why It Matters
His formulation clarifies the central distinction between one-off retrieval systems and an incrementally maintained knowledge artifact. [src-2026-04-12-001]
