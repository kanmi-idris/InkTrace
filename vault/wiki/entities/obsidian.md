# Obsidian

## Who or What
Obsidian is presented in the source as the live browsing surface where the user reads the evolving wiki while the LLM edits markdown files in the background. [src-2026-04-12-001]

## Why It Matters
It turns the wiki into a navigable graph rather than a hidden storage layer, which is why InkTrace uses an Obsidian-compatible vault as its primary interface. [src-2026-04-12-001]
