# Retrieval-Augmented Generation

## Who or What
Retrieval-augmented generation is described in the source as the dominant pattern where an LLM retrieves raw fragments at query time and synthesizes an answer on demand. [src-2026-04-12-001]

## Why It Matters
It is the baseline approach that the LLM wiki pattern is trying to improve upon by making synthesis persistent instead of ephemeral. [src-2026-04-12-001]
